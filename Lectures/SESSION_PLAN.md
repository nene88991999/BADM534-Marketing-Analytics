# Session Plan

By Nathan Yang

The historical syllabus contains 15 meetings of 170 minutes and one spring-break week. This plan preserves that amount of meeting time and allocates all instruction to the course instructor. There are no guest speakers. It contains 13 instructional meetings and two provisional presentation meetings. Dates, presentation requirements, assessment weights, and the course-wide AI policy remain unset. The ten-minute breaks below are proposed allocations.

The total is 2,550 scheduled minutes (42.5 hours), including 150 minutes of breaks. The eight modules therefore have 2,400 minutes of instruction, applied work, discussion, and provisional presentations. The [machine-readable plan](session_plan.json) records each block.

## Coverage and Course Requirements

The sequence follows M1–M8 in the [course map](../COURSE_MAP.md). The mHealth cases recur across the modules; restaurant availability and generative-AI demand supply additional comparisons. Ethics and regulation appear throughout, with a 75-minute block in Session 14. The [ethics guide](../06_Lecture_Notes/ETHICS_AND_REGULATION.md) identifies the corresponding notes and checked public sources.

The [syllabus planning document](../02_Syllabus/TEACHING_PLAN.md) records the educational structure. It does not release deadlines or graded work. Use [course.json](../course.json) to determine whether a current syllabus or schedule has been released.
