# Agent Guide for Conflicts of Interest

Read [README.md](README.md), M9 §§9.45–9.49, and the course writing guide before editing. The inputs are original synthetic scenario totals with a fixed population under each policy. Preserve all group denominators and the distinction between observed evidence, constructed outcomes, and normative premises.

- Identify the responsibility that an interest threatens. Opposing interests alone do not establish a professional conflict or misconduct.
- Preserve separate fields for commercial contribution, goal completion, regret, stopping authority, and disclosure. Do not call the weighted score consumer welfare or Scanlon's criterion.
- The equal-outcome disclosure scenario is an imposed comparison. It supplies no causal estimate of disclosure.
- Reward-learning evidence does not measure dopamine release or establish a clinical diagnosis. Link factual claims to the selected primary sources.
- Keep the complete lecture Python block synchronized with `evaluate_policies.py`. Run the calculation, independent verification, and root code-block checker after changes.
- Keep private instructor notes outside public materials. The lab is optional ungraded practice and creates no assessment requirements or authorization to experiment on people.
