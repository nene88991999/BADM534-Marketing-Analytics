# Results and Interpretation

The selection has six documents, three earnings calls, five issuer families, and ten purposeful claim records. Five documents were downloaded and machine-extracted. Southern Company's accounting passage was checked through the web reader but remains unavailable to the executed downloader. Blank text counts for that source preserve the missing observation.

The comparison matrix retains five claims concerning increases, three concerning offsets, and two describing mechanisms. These counts follow the selected coding units. They are not frequencies among utilities or estimates of public sentiment. Related claims within one source provide context and should not be counted as independent confirmations.

The New Jersey regulator's fixed-use illustration for PSE&G changes from USD 155.84 to USD 182.71 per month at 650 kWh, a USD 26.87 change (17.24%). Holding that monthly comparison for twelve months gives USD 322.44. This arithmetic describes announced auction prices; later relief, other charges, and actual usage are outside the comparison. Evergy's proposed USD 17.25 monthly increase at 1,005 kWh would annualize to USD 207 under the same fixed-month assumption if approved. The release does not supply the original bill needed to compute a comparable percentage.

A recovery mechanism can operate while new-load revenue offsets part of its cost. Neither the proposed bill nor the auction illustration assigns a causal fraction to data centers. Wholesale capacity charges and recovery of construction investment are separate channels. Their relationship to historical effects requires matched project, tariff, and outcome evidence.
