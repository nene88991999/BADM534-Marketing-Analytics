# Agent Guide for Analytical Arguments

Read the root guide, this folder's README, and M1 §1.20, M4 §4.29, M6 §6.38, and M9 §9.40.

- Preserve complete matching Python listings in M4 and M6. Run both programs, independent checks, the figure builder, and `scripts/check_lecture_code.py` after a change.
- The examples contain imposed population probabilities and valuations. Never describe their results as estimates from the pricing papers, the data-center paper, or actual customers.
- Historical prices lack within-group overlap. The two models agree on every observed cell, including the group variable. Identifying the common-price effect requires further evidence or a defensible restriction.
- Hold the population, product, information, and availability fixed in the stated intervention. A change to any of those objects defines a different comparison.
- Monetary consumption values are independently imposed. The same ranking does not determine cardinal gaps; choice probabilities do not supply authorization or an ethical conclusion.
- A counterexample must preserve the premises it challenges. Failure to establish harmlessness does not establish harm. Preserve uncertainty and the actual source's qualifications when discussing the electricity case.
- The five-step exercise and LLM prompt are optional practice, subject to applicable assessment-specific AI rules. Do not supply invented evidence, testimony, or citations.
