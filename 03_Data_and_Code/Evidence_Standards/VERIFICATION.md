# Verification

Verified November 27, 2026.

## Scope

The program calculates probabilities and losses from two stipulated normal distributions. It estimates no causal effect, demand system, market prevalence, or actual enforcement loss. Both cost scenarios use the same population share and signal distributions. Correct decisions have zero loss.

## Executed Checks

- Probability symmetry at the midpoint between the two state means.
- False-positive probability of one half at a zero threshold under the null.
- Equal-prior, equal-cost optimum at the signal midpoint.
- Analytical optima within 0.00051 of an independently enumerated grid with spacing 0.001 for both cost scenarios.
- Expected loss at each analytical optimum no greater than at the grid minimum.
- Posterior odds at the first scenario's optimum equal the ratio of false-positive to false-negative costs.
- Complete Python in the Markdown companion agrees with `error_costs.py`.
- The three plotted decision points agree with `decision_rules.csv`; figure labels use generated values.
- The TikZ figure compiled successfully and its PNG was inspected. Labels, units, assumptions, and the minimum remain readable without overlap.

Substantial changes to the range of thresholds or losses also require checking axis limits and label positions.

## Interpretation

The checks establish the calculations under the stated assumptions. They do not determine which losses an actual institution ought to use, whether harms can be placed on a common scale, or whether a particular legal standard is satisfied. Those questions require institutional evidence and moral or legal justification.
