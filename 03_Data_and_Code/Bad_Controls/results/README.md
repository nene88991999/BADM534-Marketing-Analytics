# Results and Interpretation

All results are synthetic. The CSV files retain the complete generated populations and comparison definitions.

| Comparison | Result | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Total effect with a mediator | 7 | Direct effect 1 plus mediated effect 6 |
| Mediator-adjusted slope | 1 | Direct path in the imposed unconfounded linear model |
| Collider-adjusted slope | −1 | Created association despite zero total effect |
| Pre-treatment collider slope | −1/3 | Timing alone does not justify adjustment |
| Instrument-like adjustment | 2 | Residual confounding raises a true effect of 1 |
| Adjustment for the measured confounder | 1 | Recovers the imposed effect |
| Panel comparison omitting relevant information | 5.5 | Total effect 4 plus a 1.5 untreated-trend difference |
| Panel adjustment for realized post-treatment covariate | −2 | Direct term, rather than the total effect of 4 |
| Baseline adjustment or linear untreated-covariate imputation | 4 | Recovers the total effect under the imposed conditions |
| Early-baseline unexposed-donor comparison | 10 | Completed-project total price increment |
| Early-baseline mixed-donor comparison | 7 | Subtracts a weighted donor increment of 3 |
| Opening-baseline mixed-donor comparison | 4 | Omits the earlier increment of 6 |

The full proposal-population effects are 22/3 and 10/3 dollars/MWh under the two scenarios. Their completed-project effects are 10 and −2 respectively. These are equally weighted finite-population effects with no sampling uncertainty. Neither scenario estimates the incidence of actual data-center development.
