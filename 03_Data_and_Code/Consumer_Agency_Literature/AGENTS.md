# Working With the Literature Example

Read README.md and CODEBOOK.md before classifying a paper or interpreting a result. The review is incomplete and primarily abstract-based. Preserve missing evidence, source versions, and the distinction between author findings and inferred misuse. Never characterize an author as intending to erode agency on the basis of this coding.

Use the public CSV to reproduce clustering. Do not treat unreviewed papers as zero-mechanism observations. Do not interpret cluster shares as the prevalence of harmful business practices. A statement about a specific paper must cite its DOI and review card and identify the evidence basis.

Full texts and raw source caches are excluded from Git. Do not force-add them. An institutional login allows authorized reading; it does not grant public redistribution rights. Keep credentials out of scripts and logs.
