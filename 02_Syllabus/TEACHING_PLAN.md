# Marketing Analytics Teaching Plan

By Nathan Yang

## Meeting Structure

Plan for 15 meetings of 170 minutes, with one spring-break week outside those meetings. The historical syllabus supplies the count and duration. Use 13 meetings for instruction and applied work and retain two provisional presentation meetings. There are no guest speakers. The [session plan](../05_Slides/SESSION_PLAN.md) allocates the time and links all 15 slide decks.

Calendar dates, graded presentation requirements, assessment weights, deadlines, and course-wide AI rules remain to be set. This teaching plan does not release those requirements. `current_syllabus` and `current_schedule` in [course.json](../course.json) remain null.

## Learning Objectives

Students should be able to define a marketing decision and the evidence needed to inform it; construct and assess a customer dataset; interpret descriptive, predictive, and causal results; evaluate product, targeting, and retention decisions; and reproduce an analysis with Python. AI-assisted coding is integrated into the specification, implementation, verification, and interpretation of each analysis.

Students should also be able to distinguish firm outcomes from customer value, identify consequences omitted by a commercial objective, and assess which public legal source applies to a proposed action. A defensible recommendation states the population, estimand, assumptions, uncertainty, customer consequences, and relevant regulatory scope.

## Cases and Applied Work

Use mHealth throughout the eight modules. Add restaurant-availability records for coverage, policy comparison, and forecasting, and generative-AI demand materials for public signals, subscription cohorts, and model verification. Synthetic examples provide small calculations that can be checked by hand. Each data application links complete Python, input definitions, sample restrictions, and expected output.

Ethics and regulation recur within the methods. The [module integration guide](../06_Lecture_Notes/ETHICS_AND_REGULATION.md) connects privacy, customer representation, claim substantiation, pricing, retention, review authenticity, experimentation, and delivery. Session 14 reserves 75 minutes for retention procedures, customer value, and regulatory scope. Presentation discussions include customer consequences and the strength of the evidence supporting the recommendation.

## Earnings-Call Application

Use pricing communication as a recurring application in meetings 2, 7, 10, and 14. Meeting 2 assesses missing text and document selection. Meeting 7 allocates its 25-minute worked comparison and 45-minute Python block to word exposure, dictionary interpretation, and AI-assisted checks; its application block connects these ideas to mHealth text and networks. Meeting 10 uses the event comparison within causal-design discussion. Meeting 14 incorporates pricing evidence within its existing 75-minute ethics and regulation block. The [worked example](../03_Data_and_Code/Earnings_Calls/README.md) provides the data, code, and interpretation cases. These activities fit the existing 15 meetings and do not add guest speakers or release graded requirements.
