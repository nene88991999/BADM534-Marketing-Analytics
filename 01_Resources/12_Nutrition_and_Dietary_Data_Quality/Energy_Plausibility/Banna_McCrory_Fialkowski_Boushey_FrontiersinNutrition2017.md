# Examining Plausibility of Self-Reported Energy Intake Data

Jinan C. Banna, Megan A. McCrory, Marie Kainoa Fialkowski, and Carol Boushey. *Frontiers in Nutrition* 4:45 (2017). [Original article](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnut.2017.00045). [Downloaded PDF](Banna_McCrory_Fialkowski_Boushey_FrontiersinNutrition2017.pdf).

The article compares simple energy cutoffs and methods using physiological requirements. Its tables organize definitions, inputs, strengths, and limitations. Method choice affects who remains in an analysis and how associations should be interpreted. Body-weight stability and activity information matter when relating expenditure to intake.

For mHealth, use the distinctions to separate incomplete logs, extreme days, and a hypothetical physiological screen. The presence of an observed calorie total does not establish that the day is complete. The lecture implementation compares several sample definitions and keeps the Goldberg scenario separate from real participant classification.

This companion summarizes the analytical use; it does not reproduce the paper's tables or all methods. Copyright 2017 the authors. The publisher PDF states the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY).
