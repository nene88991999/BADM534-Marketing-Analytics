# Critical Evaluation of Energy Intake Using the Goldberg Cut-off

Alison E. Black. *International Journal of Obesity* 24, 1119–1130 (2000). [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.ijo.0801376). [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11033980/).

The paper updates the variance components used in energy-intake-to-basal-metabolic-rate screening. Tables 7 and 8 distinguish group and individual bounds and show their dependence on assessment duration and activity assumptions. Individual screening has limited sensitivity; activity information is necessary for a suitable comparison. The course code checks its seven-day illustrative limits against Table 8.

The full publisher PDF is retained in the instructor's Dropbox-only resource folder. Redistribution rights were not established, so the public repository contains this original summary and links.
