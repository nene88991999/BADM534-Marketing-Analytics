## T01. Fiction and Metaphysics

(Thomasson, 1999). 1999 author bibliography; publisher chapter metadata also lists 1998. [Source](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/fiction-and-metaphysics/fictional-characters-as-abstract-artifacts/CE4968D080D4B81C9DE76FF589C70017).

**Reading focus:** Chapter 3, pp. 35–42; creation, dependence, and identity.

Fictional characters depend on creative acts and literary practices. The course applies the questions of origin, persistence, and identity to datasets, while distinguishing records from the people and events they represent.

**Access and scope:** Whole book downloaded; Chapter 3 dependency account inspected. The analogy does not establish that household bills are fictional or that every data object is an abstract entity.

## T02. Public Artifacts, Intentions and Norms

(Thomasson, 2014). 2014; Artefact Kinds, pp. 45–62. [Source](https://www.amiethomasson.org/_files/ugd/63fc8a_840cc91860474dfa99802928543989fb.doc).

**Reading focus:** Intended features and public norms of treatment.

Public artifacts can involve structural, perceptual, receptive, and normative features. A billing record is designed for a practice whose purposes and recognized uses deserve examination.

**Access and scope:** Author manuscript; opening and public-norm discussion inspected. An artifact’s intended purpose does not guarantee accuracy or legitimate every later use.

## T04. If Models Were Fictions, Then What Would They Be?

(Thomasson, 2020). 2020; The Scientific Imagination, pp. 50–73. [Source](https://www.amiethomasson.org/_files/ugd/63fc8a_793dccac983145af90b9e580203e5333.docx).

**Reading focus:** Model descriptions, model systems, and the conditional argument.

Keep the description, the constructed model, and its target distinct. A synthetic billing population can demonstrate a logical possibility without measuring its prevalence in the world.

**Access and scope:** Author manuscript downloaded; introduction and distinctions inspected. The conditional exploration does not assert that all models are fictions.

## T05. What Counts as Scientific Data? A Relational Framework

(Leonelli, 2015). 2015; Philosophy of Science 82(5), 810–821; DOI 10.1086/684083. [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4747116/).

**Reading focus:** Relational account of data and prospective evidence.

Whether an object functions as data depends on its role in inquiry and movement across settings. A ledger becomes evidence for a new claim through an evidential argument, with context and provenance retained.

**Access and scope:** Full public HTML inspected. PDF endpoint returned a verification page; no local PDF. The relational account (Leonelli, 2015) should not be reduced to the claim that every data object is itself a true or false proposition.

## T06. Saving the Phenomena

(Bogen and Woodward, 1988). 1988; The Philosophical Review 97(3), 303–352. [Source](https://joelvelasco.net/teaching/5330/bogen_woodward88-saving_the_phenomena.pdf).

**Reading focus:** Opening distinction between particular observations and phenomena.

Reliable patterns are inferred from particular records using substantive reasoning. A stable pattern in aggregate prices and a household burden mechanism are different evidential targets.

**Access and scope:** Full paper downloaded; opening distinction inspected. Their data–phenomena distinction is separate from the phenomena–noumena distinction (Kant, 1781/1787).

## T16. Learning from Data Journeys

(Leonelli and Tempini, 2020). 2020; Data Journeys in the Sciences, pp. 1–24; DOI 10.1007/978-3-030-37177-7_1. [Source](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-37177-7_1).

**Reading focus:** Production, movement, and reuse across institutions.

Linkage and portability require infrastructure and contextual knowledge. The existence of separate billing, investment, and project files does not establish that a defensible linked analysis is available.

**Access and scope:** Open chapter downloaded; introduction and data-movement argument inspected.

## T18. Social Ontology

(Epstein, 2024). Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy; revision March 4, 2024. [Source](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/social-ontology/).

**Reading focus:** Introduction and distinctions between construction and membership conditions.

Ask what establishes an institutional category and what qualifies something for membership. A valid tariff can help constitute a debt while remaining open to moral criticism.

**Access and scope:** Reference introduction and conceptual distinctions inspected; electricity example is an application.

## T19. Realism and Human Kinds

(Thomasson, 2003). *Philosophy and Phenomenological Research* 67(3), 580–609. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1933-1592.2003.tb00309.x) | [Public reading copy](https://joelvelasco.net/teaching/5330%28spring2021%29/thomasson03-human_kinds.pdf).

Read §§2–3, especially pp. 592–600. Intended features and the success of production help distinguish artifactual kinds. Apply this to the creation of a dataset, keeping accuracy of individual records and warrant for a broader claim separate. A schema's existence does not establish the truth of every entry or the adequacy of a welfare interpretation. The full-text sections were inspected; the dataset application is developed in M2 §§2.33–2.34 and M9 §§9.60–9.62.
