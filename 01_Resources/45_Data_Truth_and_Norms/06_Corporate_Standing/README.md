# Corporate Standing, Secrecy, and Responsibility

The central decision is whether a company may claim research authority while retaining control over the evidence needed to challenge it. Distinguish legal personhood, moral agency, and moral standing. Corporate duties do not automatically entail protections equivalent to human dignity. A defensible commercial restriction must identify its protected interests and justify the burden imposed on affected people.

## Maxims and Responsible Practice

State the circumstance, action, and end before judging a data practice. Evaluate universalization and respect for humanity separately from reasonable rejection (Kant, 1785, 4:400n, 4:421–429; Scanlon, 1998, chapter 5). Relevant source readings are already held in [Marketing Dialectics](../../15_Marketing_Dialectics/README.md). A hypothetical maxim supplies a philosophical comparison; attributing an actual purpose requires evidence about the actor's choices.

Responsibility for creation, access, assertion, and repair is the course's application of these philosophical arguments. The creation of an artifact does not by itself prove moral fault, and an NDA does not by itself prove suppression. The material specifies the further premises and evidence required.
