## DC01: Electricity Rate Designs for Large Loads: Evolving Practices and Opportunities

Andrew Satchwell; Natalie Mims Frick; Peter Cappers; Sanem Sergici; Ryan Hledik; Goksin Kavlak; Glenda Oskar. Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, January 2025.

[Original source](https://emp.lbl.gov/publications/electricity-rate-designs-large-loads) | [Public PDF](https://eta-publications.lbl.gov/sites/default/files/2025-01/electricity_rate_designs_for_large_loads_evolving_practices_and_opportunities_final.pdf)

The brief identifies underused infrastructure and allocation of large-load costs as contemporary regulatory concerns. Contract commitments, security, and exit arrangements determine exposure. The discussion includes pending and approved arrangements, not one national rule.

**Access and scope:** Full PDF; financial-risk discussion and date scope inspected.
