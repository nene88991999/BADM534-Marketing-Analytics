## BC17: Difference-in-Differences with Variation in Treatment Timing

Andrew Goodman-Bacon. Journal of Econometrics, 2021, 225(2), 254–277; NBER working paper 25018.

[Original source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2021.03.014) | [Public PDF](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w25018/w25018.pdf)

**Read:** Two-way fixed-effects decomposition.

With staggered timing, already-treated units can enter comparisons and contaminate interpretation under changing effects. This is a treatment-timing comparison problem, distinct from conditioning on a mediator.

**Access and scope:** NBER September 2018 working paper; abstract and decomposition inspected.

## BC18: Interpreting Pre-Trends as Anticipation: Impact on Estimated Treatment Effects from Tort Reform

Anup Malani; Julian Reif. Journal of Public Economics, 2015; author manuscript February 9, 2015.

[Original source](https://julianreif.com/research/) | [Public PDF](https://julianreif.com/research/reif.jpube.2015.anticipation.pdf)

**Read:** Anticipation and pre-treatment trends.

Behavior can change before formal treatment. A lead coefficient may reflect anticipation or another source of differential trends. Establish institutional timing before choosing the baseline or interpreting a pre-trend test.

**Access and scope:** Author manuscript dated February 9, 2015; abstract and setup inspected.

## BC19: Using Synthetic Controls: Feasibility, Data Requirements, and Methodological Aspects

Alberto Abadie. Journal of Economic Literature, 2021, 59(2), 391–425.

[Original source](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jel.20191450) | [Public PDF](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/publications/jel.20191450.pdf)

**Read:** Contextual requirements; no interference; no anticipation.

A good pre-treatment fit depends on a relevant donor pool and a period preceding exposure. Donors affected through spillovers and baselines after anticipatory responses can invalidate the counterfactual despite an appealing fit.

**Access and scope:** Published JEL article; donor, interference, and anticipation sections inspected.

## BC20: Difference-in-Differences with Spatial Spillovers

Kyle Butts. arXiv:2105.03737v3, submitted June 10, 2023; PDF dated June 13, 2023.

[Original source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03737) | [Public PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.03737)

**Read:** Spillover framework and identification.

Treatment elsewhere can change comparison outcomes and treated outcomes. The relevant neighborhood must follow the economic connection. Shared transmission systems can matter even where county boundaries or geographic distance suggest separation.

**Access and scope:** arXiv v3 submitted June 10, 2023; PDF dated June 13; setup inspected.
