# Digital Usage and Customer Access

A firm's decision to move service into an app depends on customers' available devices, tasks, and alternatives. Pereira and coauthors' geographic usage indices provide a concrete case for assessing the distance between a recorded behavior and a marketing decision.

Read the [source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md) for the article, supplement, release version, measurement scope, and unresolved numerical discrepancies. The [catalog](catalog.json) records the paper, dataset, Census data, and geographic documentation. [Rights and attribution](RIGHTS.md) accompany the archived article and supplement. The public data and original course programs are in [Digital Usage](../../03_Data_and_Code/Digital_Usage/README.md).
