Research Question:
Are any of the 13 advertising-content feature coefficients associated with loan take-up among former borrowers in a field experiment with a South African consumer lender?

Hypothesis:
H0: All 13 advertising-content feature coefficients are zero
H1: At least one advertising-content feature coefficient is nonzero

STUDY DESIGN:
- Type: Randomized field experiment
- Unit of observation: Individual borrower
- Source cohort: Former borrowers of a South African consumer lender
- Stratification: Risk category and wave
- Arms: Multiple factorial advertising content treatments

VARIABLE GLOSSARY:
- tookup: Borrower actually took up a loan (binary: yes/no in CSV) [OUTCOME]
- speak_trt: Advertisement mentions speaking to a representative (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- stripany: Mailer includes a teaser strip on the envelope (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- dphoto_none: Mailer includes no photograph (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- dphoto_black: Mailer includes a Black model photograph (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- dphoto_female: Mailer includes a female model photograph (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- gender_match: Model gender matches borrower gender (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- race_match: Model race matches borrower race (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- prize: Promotional prize mentioned in the offer (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- oneln_trt: Mailer includes a one-line message treatment (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- use_any: Mailer includes any suggested use for the loan (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- intshown: Interest rate is shown in the mailer (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- comploss_n: Comparison language about losses is included (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- comp_n: Comparison language is included (binary: yes/no in CSV) [TREATMENT]
- risk: Borrower risk category
- wave: Mailing wave
