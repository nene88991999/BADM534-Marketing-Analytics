## M01: The Market in the Kingdom of Ends: Kant’s Moral Philosophy for Business Ethics

Paolo Santori. 2024. Philosophy of Management 23:239–256; DOI 10.1007/s40926-024-00252-z

Defends a Kantian interpretation of mutually beneficial commercial exchange. Useful for evaluating a practice without assuming that a profit motive makes it intrinsically wrongful.

**Reading scope:** Abstract, introduction, and conclusion.
**Source:** [The Market in the Kingdom of Ends: Kant’s Moral Philosophy for Business Ethics](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40926-024-00252-z)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** Article CC BY 4.0; original website snapshot retained locally, including publisher navigation.

## M02: Normative Perspectives for Ethical and Socially Responsible Marketing

Gene R. Laczniak; Patrick E. Murphy. 2006. University-hosted accepted version; Journal of Macromarketing 26:154–177

## M03: The General Theory of Marketing Ethics: A Revision and Three Questions

Shelby D. Hunt; Scott J. Vitell. 2006. Journal of Macromarketing 26:143–153; DOI 10.1177/0276146706290923

Describes and revisits an explanatory model of ethical decision making. A positive account of how judgments arise differs from a justification of what ought to be done.

**Reading scope:** Publisher abstract only; file download blocked.

## M04: A Comparison of Approaches to Advertising Measurement: Evidence from Big Field Experiments at Facebook

Brett R. Gordon; Florian Zettelmeyer; Neha Bhargava; Dan Chapsky. 2018. April 12, 2018 working paper; later published in Marketing Science (2019)

Compares observational advertising estimates with randomized experiments in the studied campaigns. Distinguish selection from incremental response, then assess whether the chosen outcome answers the customer-benefit claim.

**Reading scope:** Abstract, design description, and conclusion.
**Source:** [A Comparison of Approaches to Advertising Measurement: Evidence from Big Field Experiments at Facebook](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/faculty/gordon_b/files/fb_comparison.pdf)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** local_only_copyright.

## M06: Dark Patterns at Scale: Findings from a Crawl of 11K Shopping Websites

Arunesh Mathur; Gunes Acar; Michael J. Friedman; Elena Lucherini; Jonathan Mayer; Marshini Chetty; Arvind Narayanan. 2019. arXiv author version retrieved November 6, 2026; ACM paper DOI 10.1145/3359183; saved-file checksum pins the copy

## M08: Persuasive Advertising, Autonomy, and the Creation of Desire

Roger Crisp. 1987. Journal of Business Ethics 6:413–418; DOI 10.1007/BF00382898
