## S01: Kant's Critique of Metaphysics

Michelle Grier. 2022. Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy; revision September 14, 2022

Interpretive guide to transcendental illusion, antinomies, and the positive role of systematic inquiry. Bibliography provides a route to deeper specialist scholarship.

**Reading scope:** Sections 2.1, 4, and 6.
**Source:** [Kant's Critique of Metaphysics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kant-metaphysics/)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** local_only_copyright.

## S02: Kant's Transcendental Idealism

Nicholas F. Stang. 2016. Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy; live edition at retrieval

Explains why the relation between appearances and things in themselves admits competing readings. This disagreement constrains a simplified account of the third antinomy.

**Reading scope:** Introduction and competing interpretations.
**Source:** [Kant's Transcendental Idealism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kant-transcendental-idealism/)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** local_only_copyright.
**Dropbox file:** `local_sources/Stang_SEP_TranscendentalIdealism.html`. Original retained locally; use the source link from GitHub.

## S03: Kant's Moral Philosophy

Robert Johnson; Adam Cureton. 2025. Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy; revision October 2, 2025

Clarifies rational self-legislation and the categorical imperative. A click, current preference, or consent form does not exhaust the philosophical concept of autonomy.

**Reading scope:** Introduction and autonomy/humanity discussions.
**Source:** [Kant's Moral Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kant-moral/)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** local_only_copyright.
**Dropbox file:** `local_sources/JohnsonCureton_SEP_MoralPhilosophy2025.html`. Original retained locally; use the source link from GitHub.

## S04: Hegel's Dialectics

Julie E. Maybee. 2016. Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy; live edition at retrieval

Context for distinguishing Kant’s critique from later dialectics. Avoid attributing a universal thesis–antithesis–synthesis recipe to either tradition.

**Reading scope:** Introduction and discussion of the three-step interpretation.
**Source:** [Hegel's Dialectics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/hegel-dialectics/)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** local_only_copyright.
**Dropbox file:** `local_sources/Maybee_SEP_HegelDialectics.html`. Original retained locally; use the source link from GitHub.

## S05: Kant on the Status of Ideas and Principles of Reason

Gabriele Gava. 2022. Open Philosophy 5:296–307; DOI 10.1515/opphil-2022-0202

Examines disagreement over the status of regulative ideas. The article challenges the assumption that all such ideas have the same status. Useful for separating an inquiry-guiding ideal from an established empirical finding.

**Reading scope:** Abstract, introduction, and argument on differing regulative assumptions.
**Source:** [Kant on the Status of Ideas and Principles of Reason](https://iris.unito.it/handle/2318/1855038)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** CC BY 4.0; copyright Gabriele Gava 2022; unchanged publisher PDF.
**File:** [Gava_OpenPhilosophy2022.pdf](open_access/Gava_OpenPhilosophy2022.pdf).
