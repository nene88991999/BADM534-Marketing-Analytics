## P01: Of the Independency of Parliament

David Hume. 1741. Hume Texts Online; final lifetime text, paragraphs 1–3 central

Institutional safeguards should work under pursuit of private interests. Hume explicitly distinguishes a design maxim from a factual claim that every person is dishonest.

**Reading scope:** Paragraphs 1–3, checked against full essay context.
**Source:** [Of the Independency of Parliament](https://davidhume.org/texts/emp/ip)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** local_only_site_markup.

## P02: An Inquiry into the Nature and Causes of the Wealth of Nations

Adam Smith. 1776. Public-domain edition; Book I, chapter XI, concluding discussion

Smith urges scrutiny of commercial-regulation proposals from interested merchants and manufacturers. Applying that reasoning to product claims is a contemporary extension of its original scope.

**Reading scope:** Book I, chapter XI, concluding paragraphs.
**Source:** [An Inquiry into the Nature and Causes of the Wealth of Nations](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/3300)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
