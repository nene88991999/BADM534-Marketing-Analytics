## K01: Critique of Pure Reason

Immanuel Kant. 1781/1787. J. M. D. Meiklejohn translation (1855)

Reason seeks an unconditioned explanation; critique examines the resulting illusion and the limits of theoretical cognition. Use the original argument to distinguish Kantian dialectic from ordinary disagreement.

**Reading scope:** Dialectic introduction, third antinomy, and appendix on regulative reason.
**Source:** [Critique of Pure Reason](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/4280)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** public_domain_US.

## K02: Prolegomena to Any Future Metaphysics

Immanuel Kant. 1783. Paul Carus translation; edition recorded in downloaded title page

A shorter route into the antinomies and the distinction between appearances and things in themselves. The mathematical and dynamical antinomies receive different resolutions.

**Reading scope:** Sections 50–54.
**Source:** [Prolegomena to Any Future Metaphysics](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/52821)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** public_domain_US.
**File:** [Kant_Prolegomena_Carus.html](public_domain_texts/Kant_Prolegomena_Carus.html).

## K03: Fundamental Principles of the Metaphysic of Morals

Immanuel Kant. 1785. Thomas Kingsmill Abbott translation; Groundwork

The Groundwork supplies the ethical principles used to assess the treatment of customers. Keep practical ethics distinct from the first Critique’s critique of metaphysics.

**Reading scope:** Second section: universal law, humanity, autonomy.
**Source:** [Fundamental Principles of the Metaphysic of Morals](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5682)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** public_domain_US.

## K04: Critique of Practical Reason

Immanuel Kant. 1788. Thomas Kingsmill Abbott translation

Examines the highest good and the relation between virtue and happiness. Avoid inferring a guarantee that ethical businesses earn higher profits.

**Reading scope:** Book II, chapter II, antinomy and critical solution.
**Source:** [Critique of Practical Reason](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5683)

## K05: Critique of Judgement

Immanuel Kant. 1790. J. H. Bernard translation; second edition 1914

Adds independent thinking, the standpoint of others, and the antinomy of taste. A preference survey is a different object from Kant’s aesthetic judgment.

**Reading scope:** Sections 40 and 55–57; contents for teleological dialectic.
**Source:** [Critique of Judgement](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/48433)
**Access:** full; downloaded.
**Rights:** public_domain_US.
