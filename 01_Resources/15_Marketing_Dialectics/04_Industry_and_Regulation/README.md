## I01: Statement of Ethics

American Marketing Association. undated. Live professional statement at retrieval; aspirational, not legislation

Professional statement with aspirational commitments. It informs discussion of responsibilities but is neither legislation nor an enforcement decision.

**Reading scope:** Ethical norms and values.
**Source:** [Statement of Ethics](https://www.ama.org/ama-statement-of-ethics/)

## I02: Bringing Dark Patterns to Light

**Source:** [Bringing Dark Patterns to Light](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/bringing-dark-patterns-light)
