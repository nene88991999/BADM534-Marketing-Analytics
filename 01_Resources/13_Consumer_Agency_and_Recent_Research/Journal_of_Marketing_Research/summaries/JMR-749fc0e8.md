# When Words Meet Visuals: How Content Composition Drives Social Media Engagement for Marketer-Generated Content

Ivy Chu Dang; Canice M.C. Kwan; Jayson S. Jia; Yang Shi. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251373042).

## Managerial Implications

The balance of pictures and overlaid text affects social engagement, with important interactions involving informativeness, placement, emotion, and visual complexity. Evaluate the complete composition and acknowledge the assumptions behind observational causal estimates.

## Consumer Agency

Visual emphasis can make promotional content salient while material conditions fade. Evaluate comprehension as well as engagement.
