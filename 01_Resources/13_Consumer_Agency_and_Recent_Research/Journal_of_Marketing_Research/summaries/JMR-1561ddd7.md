# TV Advertising and Online Sales: A Case Study of Intertemporal Substitution Effects for an Online Travel Platform

Anja Lambrecht; Catherine Tucker; Xu Zhang. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231180171).

Online publication: 2023-08-01. Issue publication: 2024-04.

## Managerial Implications

TV advertising can bring online browsing and sales forward in time. Very short attribution windows can therefore overstate incremental demand. Evaluate later offsetting declines and channel changes before allocating advertising spending.
