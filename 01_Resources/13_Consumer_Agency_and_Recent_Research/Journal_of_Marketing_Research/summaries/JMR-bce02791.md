# The Daily Me Versus the Daily Others: How Do Recommendation Algorithms Change User Interests? Evidence from a Knowledge-Sharing Platform

Jia Liu; Ziwei Cong. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437221134237).

Online publication: 2023-02-12. Issue publication: 2023-08.

## Managerial Implications

Moving from content-based to social recommendations changes participation and the concentration of interests in different directions. Platforms should distinguish social-network concentration from topic diversity when evaluating recommendation changes.

## Consumer Agency

Recommendation rules shape exposure and expressed interests. Give users control over recommendation criteria and access to alternative content.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: personalization, social_cues, delegation_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
