# The Last Hurrah Effect: End-of-Period Temporal Landmarks Increase Optimism and Financial Risk-Taking

Avni M. Shah; Xinlong Li. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437241286785).

Online publication: 2025-02-10. Issue publication: 2025-04.

## Managerial Implications

End-of-period landmarks can increase optimism and financial risk-taking, with worse subsequent returns in the studied lending setting. Financial communication should account for decision timing and assess outcomes beyond immediate participation.

## Consumer Agency

Time-linked optimism can expose consumers to excessive risk. Provide stable risk information and time to reconsider.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: emotion_identity, financial_commitment. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
