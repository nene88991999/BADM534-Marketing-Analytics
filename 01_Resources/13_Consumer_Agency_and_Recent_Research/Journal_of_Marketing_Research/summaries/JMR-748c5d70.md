# The Weekend Effect in Online Reviews

Andreas Bayerl; Verena Schoenmueller; Jacob Goldenberg; Florian Stahl. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251391808).

Online publication: 2026-02-12. Issue publication: 2026-04.

## Managerial Implications

Weekend reviews differ systematically from weekday reviews, partly because different reviewers choose to participate. Review-solicitation timing can therefore change observed ratings. Managers should account for temporal selection before treating score changes as changes in quality.

## Consumer Agency

Selective solicitation timing can manufacture a more favorable apparent customer experience. Report solicitation practices and maintain representative feedback access.
