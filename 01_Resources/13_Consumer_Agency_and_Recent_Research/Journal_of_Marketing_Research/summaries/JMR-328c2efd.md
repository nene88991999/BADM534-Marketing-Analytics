# The Different Effects of Mass-Media Marketing and Personal Sales Budgets Across the Life Cycle of B2B High-Tech Start-Ups

Arnd Vomberg; Maximilian Friess; Sascha Alavi; Verena Maag; Jan Wieseke. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251367766).

Online publication: 2025-11-21. Issue publication: 2026-02.

## Managerial Implications

The association between sales-versus-media spending and performance changes over a B2B startup's life cycle. Personal selling is particularly useful early, while mass media becomes more relevant later. Budget decisions should reflect maturity and the performance measure being pursued.
