# Is It as Bad as It Looks? Judgments of Quantitative Scores Depend on Their Presentation Format

Christophe Lembregts; Jeroen Schepers; Arne De Keyser. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231193343).

Online publication: 2023-10-23. Issue publication: 2024-10.

## Managerial Implications

Cumulative versus episode-level score displays can change how people interpret an unusual rating. Cumulative displays may obscure the severity of a deviation. Platforms should provide understandable raw observations and aggregation rules when consequential decisions depend on ratings.

## Consumer Agency

An aggregation format can distort inference about an underlying episode while displaying numerically correct data. Make both components and averages accessible.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: framing_reference, opacity_disclosure. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
