# Rewarding Money or Time? The Sunk Cost Effects of Customized Rewards in Loyalty Programs

Zhigang Shou; Qinying Xia; Samuel Su; Hongxin Teng. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251353156).

Online publication: 2025-10-09. Issue publication: 2026-04.

## Managerial Implications

Unexpected rewards for time and money are interpreted differently. Consumers may undercount time costs and treat time-based rewards as windfalls. Loyalty evaluation should include opportunity costs and subsequent spending, alongside redemption and gratitude.

## Consumer Agency

Reward framing can conceal accumulated effort and encourage further spending. Display time and monetary commitments transparently.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: habits_rewards, financial_commitment, framing_reference. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
