# The Effects of Content Ephemerality on Information Processing

Uri Barnea; Robert J. Meyer; Gideon Nave. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437221131047).

Online publication: 2023-02-27. Issue publication: 2023-08.

## Managerial Implications

Ephemeral content can increase immediate attention, comprehension, and recall by raising concern about missing information. However, customers lose opportunities for later review. Consequential terms should remain accessible even when promotional content is temporary.

## Consumer Agency

Restricting repeat access can impair later reconsideration and verification despite improved initial attention. Preserve retrievable records.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: attention_salience, exit_search_friction. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
