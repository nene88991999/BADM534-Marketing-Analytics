# The Token-Effort Effect: Trivial Redemption Effort Increases Price Promotion Effectiveness

Kuangjie Zhang; Thomas Allard; Nidhi Agrawal; Rajesh Bagchi. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251338050).

Online publication: 2025-07-15. Issue publication: 2025-12.

## Managerial Implications

Very small redemption tasks can increase promotion uptake by creating a favorable perceived return on effort. More demanding tasks do not share that effect. Managers should evaluate participation costs and avoid using nominal effort to distract from the underlying offer.

## Consumer Agency

A trivial task can change valuation of an unchanged discount. Keep the price comparison clear and participation voluntary.
