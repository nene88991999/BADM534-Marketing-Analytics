# Driving Mobile App User Engagement Through Gamification

Jens W. Paschmann; Hernán A. Bruno; Harald J. van Heerde; Franziska Völckner; Kristina Klein. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437241275927).

Online publication: 2024-11-26. Issue publication: 2025-04.

## Managerial Implications

Game rewards can raise app engagement beyond ordinary rewards, but intensive game involvement may contribute less to the app's substantive activity. Evaluate useful participation separately from time spent and reward pursuit.

## Consumer Agency

Reward progression can sustain activity after it stops serving the user's objective. Offer limits, pauses, and transparent reward rules.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: habits_rewards, attention_salience. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
