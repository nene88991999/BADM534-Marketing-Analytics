# Bots Bargaining with Humans: Building AI Super-Bargainers with Algorithmic Anthropomorphization

Sumon Chaudhuri; Arnaud De Bruyn. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251375323).

Online publication: 2026-01-16. Issue publication: 2026-04.

## Managerial Implications

Bargaining bots can improve economic outcomes while appearing human, yet damage subjective negotiation experiences. Managers should evaluate relationship consequences as well as immediate gains. Clear agent identity and appropriate negotiation safeguards are essential.

## Consumer Agency

Humanlike behavior can obscure the counterparty's automated incentives. Disclose automation and preserve recourse and refusal.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: delegation_control, social_cues, opacity_disclosure. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
