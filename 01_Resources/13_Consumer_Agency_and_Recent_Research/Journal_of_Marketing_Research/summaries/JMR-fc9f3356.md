# Save More Today or Tomorrow: The Role of Urgency in Precommitment Design

Joseph Reiff; Hengchen Dai; John Beshears; Katherine L. Milkman; Shlomo Benartzi. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231153396).

Online publication: 2023-06-02. Issue publication: 2023-12.

## Managerial Implications

The order of immediate and later commitment options changes inferred urgency and saving behavior. Simultaneous offers can weaken perceived urgency, while sequential offers can strengthen it. Evaluate informed commitment and retained flexibility.

## Consumer Agency

Sequencing can imply urgency beyond the underlying choice. Explain timing consequences and allow deliberate deferral.
