# Overestimating Stars, Underestimating Numbers: The Hidden Impact of Rating Formats

Deepak Sirwani; Srishti Kumar; Manoj Thomas. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251322425).

Online publication: 2025-05-15. Issue publication: 2025-10.

## Managerial Implications

Stars and numeric ratings can generate different systematic magnitude errors. Partial-star rendering and left-digit attention help explain them. Platforms should test comprehension and provide readable underlying values rather than presume familiar formats are neutral.

## Consumer Agency

Display format can bias evaluation of identical data. Use interpretable scales and make exact values available.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: framing_reference, attention_salience. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
