# Soda Taxes and Marketing Conduct

Kristopher O. Keller; Jonne Y. Guyt; Rajdeep Grewal. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231195551).

Online publication: 2023-10-27. Issue publication: 2024-06.

## Managerial Implications

Soda-tax responses include changes in promotion frequency, discount depth, and feature advertising. These changes can reinforce sales declines beyond the price effect alone. Policy and brand analyses should model the broader marketing mix and changes in consumer responsiveness.
