# Market Effects of Inattention: Theory and Evidence from Left-Digit Bias

Andreas Kraft; Raghunath Singh Rao. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437261427475).

Online publication: 2026-05-21. Issue publication: 2026-10.

## Managerial Implications

Firms can profit from consumer inattention even in competitive used-goods markets. Used-car evidence links odometer left-digit bias to dealer transactions and margins. Customer protection should address how information is interpreted as well as whether it is available.

## Consumer Agency

Targeting biased interpretation can transfer surplus while formal information remains available. Present comparable quantities and test comprehension.

Assessment: reported choice concern. Mechanism features: attention_salience, framing_reference, personalization. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
