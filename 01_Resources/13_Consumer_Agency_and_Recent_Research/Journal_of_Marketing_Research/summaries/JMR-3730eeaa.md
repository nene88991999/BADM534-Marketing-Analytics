# The Effects of Pressure and Self-Assurance Nudges on Product Purchases and Returns in Online Retailing: Evidence from a Randomized Field Experiment

Anindya Ghose; Heeseung Andrew Lee; Kihwan Nam; Wonseok Oh. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231180494).

Online publication: 2023-08-09. Issue publication: 2024-06.

## Managerial Implications

Pressure and reassurance can both raise sales, but pressure-oriented nudges generate more product returns in the field experiment. Return-adjusted sales favor a broader evaluation than immediate conversion. Product-fit support can preserve commercial performance while improving decision quality.

## Consumer Agency

Scarcity and social pressure can prompt purchases later reversed. Measure returns and regret and support informed product-fit assessment.
