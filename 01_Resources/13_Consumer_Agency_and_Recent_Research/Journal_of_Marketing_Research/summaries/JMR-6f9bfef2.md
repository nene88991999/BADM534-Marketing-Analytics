# The Impact of Negative Reviews on Online Search and Purchase Decisions

Marton Varga; Paulo Albuquerque. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231190874).

Online publication: 2023-09-06. Issue publication: 2024-10.

## Managerial Implications

Negative reviews can change search paths as well as purchase probability, particularly for functional products and complaints about functionality or service. Retailers should diagnose the underlying issue and offer representative review access, rather than interpret a mean rating as sufficient information.

## Consumer Agency

Selective review visibility can hide information that would change search and purchase. Preserve representative access and clear sorting rules.
