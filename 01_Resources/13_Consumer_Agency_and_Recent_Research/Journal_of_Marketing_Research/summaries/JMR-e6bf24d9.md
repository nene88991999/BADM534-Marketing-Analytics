# How Do Nutritional Warning Labels Affect Prices?

Max J. Pachali; Marco J.W. Kotschedoff; Arjen van Lin; Bart J. Bronnenberg; Erica van Herpen. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437221105014).

Online publication: 2022-09-06. Issue publication: 2023-02.

## Managerial Implications

Nutrition-warning policies can change firms' prices as well as consumers' preferences. In the Chilean cereal setting, price adjustment reinforces substitution toward healthier products. Evaluate the equilibrium response and effects on price-sensitive households rather than hold all prices fixed when assessing policy.

## Consumer Agency

Warning labels improve access to consequential information. The welfare interpretation also depends on price responses and consumers' needs.
