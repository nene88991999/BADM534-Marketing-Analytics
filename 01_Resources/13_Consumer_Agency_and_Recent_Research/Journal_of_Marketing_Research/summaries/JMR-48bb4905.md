# Consumer Interactions and Peer Effects in Socially Connected Digital Products

Yijun Chen; Yulia Nevskaya. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437261463518).

Online publication: 2026-06-16. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Peer effects can materially alter retention intervention value in socially connected products. A structural model accounts for endogenous network formation and heterogeneous users. Customer-value calculations should distinguish direct retention value from estimated network spillovers.

## Consumer Agency

Retention interventions can create social pressure to remain. Preserve independent exit and avoid making peers responsible for another user's participation.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: social_cues, habits_rewards, personalization. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
