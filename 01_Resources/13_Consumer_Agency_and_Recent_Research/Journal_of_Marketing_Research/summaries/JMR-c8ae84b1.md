# Who Shares Fake News? Uncovering Insights from Social Media Users’ Post Histories

Verena Schoenmueller; Simon J. Blanchard; Gita Venkataramani Johar. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437241281873).

Online publication: 2024-12-16. Issue publication: 2025-04.

## Managerial Implications

Past social-media language helps identify misinformation-sharing patterns and design fact-checking interventions. The studies distinguish trait from situational anger and test an intervention encouraging tool adoption. Prediction should be evaluated for error, privacy, and the specific behavior measured.

## Consumer Agency

Profiles intended for protective intervention could be repurposed to target susceptible users with misinformation. Restrict data use and evaluate protective outcomes.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: personalization, emotion_identity, privacy_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
