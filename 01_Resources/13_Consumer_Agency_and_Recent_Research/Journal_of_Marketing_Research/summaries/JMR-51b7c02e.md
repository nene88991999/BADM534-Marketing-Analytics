# Taking a Stance, for Now or Forever: Optimizing the Communication of Corporate Political Activism

Freeman Wu; Helen van der Sluis; Lauren Grewal; Aradhna Krishna. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437261470421).

Online publication: 2026-07-07. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Communication permanence can signal commitment to a political stance, with responses depending on audience alignment and other evidence of commitment. Managers should align durable communications with substantive organizational action.

## Consumer Agency

Identity-aligned commitment signals can win support without equivalent action. Make underlying practices verifiable.
