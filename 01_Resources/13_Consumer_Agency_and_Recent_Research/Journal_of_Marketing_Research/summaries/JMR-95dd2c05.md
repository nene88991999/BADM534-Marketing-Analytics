# Separating the Artist from the Art: Social Media Boycotts, Platform Sanctions, and Music Consumption

Daniel Winkler; Nils Wlömert; Jūra Liaukonytė. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437261445841).

Online publication: 2026-04-13. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Reduced platform visibility can explain consumption declines that appear to reflect consumer boycotts. Separating playlist removal from intentional audience response changes the interpretation. Managers should identify the source of demand changes before attributing them to values or preferences.

## Consumer Agency

Platform curation can shape feasible exposure while observed consumption is mistaken for autonomous preference. Provide discoverable alternatives and transparent curation.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: attention_salience, delegation_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
