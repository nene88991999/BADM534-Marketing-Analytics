# Response Aggregation to Obtain Truthful Answers to Sensitive Questions: Estimating the Prevalence of Illegal Purchases of Prescription Drugs

Marco Gregori; Martijn G. De Jong; Rik Pieters. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231205252).

Online publication: 2023-12-13. Issue publication: 2024-10.

## Managerial Implications

Aggregating responses to paired questions can reduce the burden of disclosing sensitive behavior and improve prevalence measurement. Analysts need the technique's statistical assumptions and should report the inferred population rate rather than identify particular respondents.

## Consumer Agency

Privacy-preserving measurement can improve participation and truthful reporting. Maintain the distinction between aggregate inference and individual disclosure.

Assessment: protective. Mechanism features: privacy_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
