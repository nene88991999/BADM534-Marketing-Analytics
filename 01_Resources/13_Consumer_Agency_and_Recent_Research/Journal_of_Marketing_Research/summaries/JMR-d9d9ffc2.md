# Neural Signals of Video Advertisement Liking: Insights into Psychological Processes and Their Temporal Dynamics

Hang-Yee Chan; Maarten A.S. Boksem; Vinod Venkatraman; Roeland C. Dietvorst; Christin Scholz; Khoi Vo; Emily B. Falk; Ale Smidts. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231194319).

Online publication: 2023-09-29. Issue publication: 2024-10.

## Managerial Implications

Neural responses during advertisements can improve predictions of subsequent liking and identify differences in response timing. These measures should be validated on new ads and audiences. Predicting liking does not establish persuasion, purchase effects, or welfare consequences.

## Consumer Agency

Optimizing early affective responses can prioritize attraction over comprehension. Include understanding and informed preference in evaluation.
