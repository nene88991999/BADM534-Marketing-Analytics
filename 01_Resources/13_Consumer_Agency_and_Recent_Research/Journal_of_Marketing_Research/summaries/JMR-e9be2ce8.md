# Information Disclosure via Platform Endorsement in Online Health Care

Jiajia Zhan; Xu Zhang; Hongqiao Fu. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251408776).

Online publication: 2026-03-29. Issue publication: 2026-08.

## Managerial Implications

Platform endorsement can increase paid consultation demand and prices while reducing free provision. The observed quality response differs from the access-equity concern. Health platforms should evaluate information value, provider behavior, and access together.

## Consumer Agency

An endorsement can be interpreted as neutral advice despite platform revenue incentives. Explain criteria and preserve visibility of suitable alternatives.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: social_cues, opacity_disclosure. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
