# EXPRESS: Infinite-Scrolling vs. Pagination: The Impact of Interface Layouts on Product Returns

Huitian Zhang; Lei Su; Jaideep Sengupta. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437261494550).

Online publication: 2026-09-23. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Infinite scrolling can increase product returns by reducing perceived navigational control and choice closure. Navigation aids and other interventions can mitigate the response. Evaluate returns and control alongside immediate browsing engagement.

## Consumer Agency

Interface design can reduce control over navigation and later confidence in choices. Provide visible position, stopping points, and easy revisiting.

Assessment: reported choice concern. Mechanism features: delegation_control, exit_search_friction, habits_rewards. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
