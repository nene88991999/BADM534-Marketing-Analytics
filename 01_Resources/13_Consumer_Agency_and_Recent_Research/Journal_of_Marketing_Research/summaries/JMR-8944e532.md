# Rejections Are Stickier Than Choices

Minzhe Xu; Yang Yang; Chris Janiszewski. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437241301417).

Online publication: 2025-02-26. Issue publication: 2025-06.

## Managerial Implications

Rejecting alternatives can create more persistent preferences than choosing among them, reducing variety in later decisions. The welfare effect depends on whether the person initially seeks too much or too little variety. Evaluate repeated choice and reconsideration.

## Consumer Agency

The response task can reshape later preference without changing attributes. Allow alternative framing and revisiting past decisions.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: defaults_order, framing_reference. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
