# Accounting for Formative and Reflective Topics in Product Review Data for Better Consumer Insights

Joachim Büschken; Thomas Otter; Greg M. Allenby. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251315745).

Online publication: 2025-04-21. Issue publication: 2025-12.

## Managerial Implications

Review topics that describe an overall evaluation can be consequences of satisfaction rather than drivers of it. Distinguishing these roles can improve topic coherence and managerial diagnosis. Avoid interpreting every predictive phrase as an actionable product attribute.
