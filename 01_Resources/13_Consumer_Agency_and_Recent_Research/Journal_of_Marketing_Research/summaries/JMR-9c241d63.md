# How Insurance Prices Affect Consumers’ Purchase Decisions: Insurance Price as a Risk Signal

Jochen Reiner; Julia Wamsler; Torsten Bornemann; Martin Natter. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437241270217).

Online publication: 2024-10-17. Issue publication: 2025-02.

## Managerial Implications

Customers may read an optional insurance price as information about the underlying product's risk. High relative insurance prices can therefore deter the product purchase itself. Provide objective risk information and evaluate the product and insurance offers jointly.

## Consumer Agency

An insurance price can alter perceived risk without changing objective risk. Clear evidence about coverage and actual failure rates supports informed choice.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: framing_reference, opacity_disclosure. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
