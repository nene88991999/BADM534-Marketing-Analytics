# The Value of Safety Training for Business-to-Business Firms

Yixing Chen; Shrihari Sridhar; Kyuhong Han; Sonam Singh; Vikas Mittal; Taehoon Im. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231222857).

Online publication: 2024-03-07. Issue publication: 2024-08.

## Managerial Implications

Safety training can reduce hazards and injuries and improve how procurement professionals evaluate a supplier. The studies combine quasi-experimental evidence and a stated-choice experiment. Training decisions should track actual safety outcomes alongside commercial benefits.

## Consumer Agency

The evidence concerns workplace safety and purchasing criteria rather than an agency-eroding mechanism.
