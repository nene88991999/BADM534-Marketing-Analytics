# Sunk Cost Effect, Self-Control, and Contract Design

Xing Zhang; Ganesh Iyer; Xiaoyan Xu; Juin Kuan Chong. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231196824).

Online publication: 2023-12-26. Issue publication: 2024-10.

## Managerial Implications

Sunk costs can supply desired commitment while reducing flexibility. Optimal contracts depend on consumers' self-control problems and the psychological mechanism. Refundable fees and alternative tariffs change the trade-off.

## Consumer Agency

A commitment device can become a trap when preferences or circumstances change. Offer understandable terms and appropriate exit or refund options.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: financial_commitment, habits_rewards. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
