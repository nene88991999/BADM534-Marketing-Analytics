# Buying Future Endorsements from Prospective Influencers on User-Generated Content Platforms

Andreas Lanz; Jacob Goldenberg; Daniel Shapira; Florian Stahl. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437231207323).

Online publication: 2024-01-04. Issue publication: 2024-10.

## Managerial Implications

Predicting which new creators will later become influential can support earlier endorsement contracting. The rare-event problem and assumed endorsement returns strongly affect the business case. Managers should validate forecasts and contractual terms before relying on simulated profit potential.

## Consumer Agency

Early commercial arrangements may be invisible to later audiences. Disclose paid relationships when endorsements occur.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: social_cues, personalization. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
