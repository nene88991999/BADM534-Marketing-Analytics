# EXPRESS: The Humanizing Power of Accents in Voicebots: When Accent-Based Humanization Increases User Engagement

Hesam Ghasemi; Amir Sepehri; Cait Lamberton. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437261494934).

Online publication: 2026-09-23. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Nonstandard accents can make voicebots seem more human and increase engagement under particular conditions. The effect depends on whether humanization is desired and whether other cues dominate. Managers should preserve accurate agent identity.

## Consumer Agency

Humanization can encourage unwarranted interpersonal trust in automation. Identify the system and explain what it can and cannot decide.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: social_cues, delegation_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
