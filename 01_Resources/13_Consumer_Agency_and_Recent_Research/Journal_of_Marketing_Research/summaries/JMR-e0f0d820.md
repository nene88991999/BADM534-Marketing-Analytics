# Improving the Discriminant Validation of Multi-Item Scales

Constant Pieters; Hans Baumgartner; Rik Pieters. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251388994).

Online publication: 2026-02-25. Issue publication: 2026-06.

## Managerial Implications

Multi-item scales require evidence that purportedly distinct constructs are empirically distinguishable. The proposed procedure incorporates reliability, measurement error, and sampling error. Analysts should evaluate discriminant validity continuously rather than rely on a convenient binary criterion.
