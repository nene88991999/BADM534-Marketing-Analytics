# Wait for Free: A Consumption-Decelerating Promotion for Serialized Digital Media

Jangwon Choi; Inyoung Chae; Fred Feinberg. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437241270194).

Online publication: 2024-10-16. Issue publication: 2025-02.

## Managerial Implications

Waiting for free access can expand a serialized content audience and monetize willingness to avoid delay. Revenue gains depend on spillovers and heterogeneity. Evaluate spending transparency and users' ability to stop between episodes.

## Consumer Agency

Serial access and paid delay avoidance can encourage escalating commitments. Show cumulative cost and support voluntary stopping.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: habits_rewards, financial_commitment, exit_search_friction. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
