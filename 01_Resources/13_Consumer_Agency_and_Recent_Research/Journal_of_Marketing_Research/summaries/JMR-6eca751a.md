# A Comparative Analysis of Frontline Employee Wellness Benefits and Customer Responsiveness: A Social Exchange Theory Perspective

Stephanie M. Noble; Dhruv Grewal; Riley T. Krotz; Carl-Philip Ahlbom; Jens Nordfält; Dipayan Biswas. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251384248).

Online publication: 2026-02-25. Issue publication: 2026-04.

## Managerial Implications

Some wellness benefits improve frontline responsiveness through feeling valued and indebted. Food and social benefits show stronger effects in the reported studies, especially in supportive workplaces. Managers should evaluate employee well-being directly rather than assume responsiveness fully captures the benefit.

## Consumer Agency

Indebtedness may turn a benefit into pressure to reciprocate. Keep benefits unconditional and preserve employee voice and reasonable work boundaries.
