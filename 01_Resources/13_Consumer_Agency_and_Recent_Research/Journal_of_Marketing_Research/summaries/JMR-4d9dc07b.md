# Product-Harm Crises and Spillover Effects: A Case Study of the Volkswagen Diesel Emissions Scandal in eBay Used Car Auction Markets

Xiaogang Che; Hajime Katayama; Peter Lee. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437221115616).

Online publication: 2022-11-18. Issue publication: 2023-04.

## Managerial Implications

A product-harm scandal can reduce demand for a firm's unaffected products. Used-car auction evidence shows negative spillovers to compliant Volkswagen models. Crisis management and valuation should account for brand-wide trust effects, while separating these from changes in marketplace participation.

## Consumer Agency

The study measures reputational spillovers rather than a method of influencing individual agency.
