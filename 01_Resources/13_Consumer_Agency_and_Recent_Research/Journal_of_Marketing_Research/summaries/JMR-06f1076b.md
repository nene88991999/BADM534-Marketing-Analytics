# The Golden Halo of Defaults in Simple Choices

Nicolette J. Sullivan; Alexander Breslav; Samyukta S. Doré; Matthew D. Bachman; Scott A. Huettel. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437241303738).

Online publication: 2025-02-11. Issue publication: 2025-04.

## Managerial Implications

Defaults can change attention and valuation in simple choices, including increasing hedonic selection. Effects depend on goals and the type of default. Designers should compare defaulted and active-choice experiences and make alternatives easy to select.

## Consumer Agency

A preselected option can acquire perceived value beyond its attributes. Transparent defaults and low-cost override preserve control.
