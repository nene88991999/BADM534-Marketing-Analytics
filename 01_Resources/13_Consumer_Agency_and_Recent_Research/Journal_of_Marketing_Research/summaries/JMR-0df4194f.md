# Gaming the Marketplace: Consumer Opportunism and Beliefs about Societal Hierarchy

Shilpa Madan; Aparna Labroo; Constantine S. Katsikeas. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437261477110).

Online publication: 2026-08-03. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Beliefs about hierarchy can encourage opportunistic consumer behavior through a desire to feel clever. Surveillance reminders and relationship reminders have different effects. Policy design should examine actual behavior and fairness rather than rely on a single relational appeal.

## Consumer Agency

Surveillance cues can create compliance pressure and inhibit legitimate claims. Explain monitoring and maintain accessible appeal and return processes.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: social_cues, privacy_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
