# How Awe in Marketing Communications Decreases Consumer Preferences for Limited Edition Products

Aysu Senyuz; Jonathan Hasford; Ze Wang. Journal of Marketing Research. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222437251333742).

Online publication: 2025-07-21. Issue publication: 2025-10.

## Managerial Implications

Awe can reduce demand for limited editions by weakening the desire for exclusivity. Message framing can alter that relationship. Campaign decisions should account for the motive a communication activates rather than assume all positive emotions increase demand.

## Consumer Agency

Emotion and exclusivity framing can redirect evaluation independently of functional value. Keep scarcity claims truthful and allow deliberation.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: emotion_identity, framing_reference. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
