# Optimizing User Engagement Through Adaptive Ad Sequencing

Omid Rafieian. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.1423).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2023-09.

## Managerial Implications

Adaptive ad sequencing can improve measured ad response while accounting for dynamic trade-offs. In the reported application, gains mainly arise from response per impression rather than increased user activity. Evaluate causal validity and customer outcomes alongside engagement.

## Consumer Agency

Adaptive sequences can optimize influence without optimizing user goals. Include stopping, comprehension, and user-selected limits in evaluation.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: personalization, defaults_order, habits_rewards. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
