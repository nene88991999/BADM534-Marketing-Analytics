# Is Competition Only One Click Away? The Digital Markets Act’s Impact on Google Maps

Louis-Daniel Pape; Michelangelo Rossi. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2025.0159).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2026-05.

## Managerial Implications

Removing direct map links from search results increased additional searches but generated little traffic for competing mapping services in the studied setting. Interface compliance and competitive outcomes can diverge. Evaluate actual access paths and feasible alternatives alongside the interface change.

## Consumer Agency

The evidence cautions that changing a link may not restore meaningful alternatives when habits and limited substitutes persist.

Assessment: protective. Mechanism features: attention_salience, exit_search_friction. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
