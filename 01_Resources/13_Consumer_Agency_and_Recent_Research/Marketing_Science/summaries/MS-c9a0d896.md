# Frontiers: Can Large Language Models Capture Human Preferences?

Ali Goli; Amandeep Singh. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0306).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2024-07.

## Managerial Implications

Language-model responses can differ materially from human intertemporal preferences. Prompting changes may reduce discrepancies without eliminating them. Use such outputs for hypothesis development and validate against people before treating simulated responses as customer demand.

## Consumer Agency

The paper warns against substituting synthetic respondents for evidence about human preferences.
