# Call Me Maybe: Does Customer Feedback Seeking Impact Nonsolicited Customers?

Rupali Kaul; Stephen J. Anderson; Pradeep K. Chintagunta; Naufel Vilcassim. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0324).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2025-01.

## Managerial Implications

Seeking feedback can improve the business itself, benefiting customers who were never solicited. The randomized Rwanda study finds spillovers consistent with implemented improvements. Managers should track operational changes and unsurveyed customers alongside direct responses to surveys.

## Consumer Agency

Using customer voice to improve offerings can support meaningful participation.
