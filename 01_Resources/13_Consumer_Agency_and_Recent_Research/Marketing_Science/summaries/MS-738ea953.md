# Welfare Effects of Personalized Rankings

Robert Donnelly; Ayush Kanodia; Ilya Morozov. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.1441).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2024-01.

## Managerial Implications

A retailer's personalized ranking can place weight on profitability while still improving estimated consumer surplus relative to a bestseller ranking. The experiment and structural model demonstrate why commercial optimization alone does not establish harm. Evaluate the consumer benchmark and modeling assumptions explicitly.

## Consumer Agency

This is a counterexample to equating personalization with harm. Alternative ranking objectives should be assessed against consumer-relevant outcomes.

Assessment: protective. Mechanism features: personalization, attention_salience. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
