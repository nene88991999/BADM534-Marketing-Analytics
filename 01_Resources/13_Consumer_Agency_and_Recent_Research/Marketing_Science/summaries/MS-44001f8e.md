# The Value of Platform Endorsement

Mimansa Bairathi; Xu Zhang; Anja Lambrecht. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.0226).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2025-01.

## Managerial Implications

Platform endorsements can increase search and purchases for both endorsed and unendorsed services by changing beliefs about overall platform quality. Evaluate spillovers and customer heterogeneity when assessing an endorsement program.

## Consumer Agency

An endorsement's credibility can spill beyond the evaluated item. Explain selection criteria and avoid implying guarantees the evidence does not support.
