# Frontiers: The Impact of Loosening Concealed Carry Laws on Firearm Demand

Jessica Jumee Kim; Yu-Chang Chen. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0480).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2025-05.

## Managerial Implications

Different changes to concealed-carry law have different demand responses in the studied period. The analysis separates permit standards, repeat purchases, and geographic heterogeneity. Policy evaluation should avoid treating all deregulation as an equivalent intervention.
