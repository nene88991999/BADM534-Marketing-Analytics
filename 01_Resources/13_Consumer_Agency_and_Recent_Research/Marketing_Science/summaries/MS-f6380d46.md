# Regulating Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) May Harm Consumers

Behnam Mohammadi; Nikhil Malik; Tim Derdenger; Kannan Srinivasan. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.0396).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2025-05.

## Managerial Implications

A game-theoretic model shows that mandatory explainability can change competition and welfare in ways that are not uniformly beneficial. Policy evaluation should specify costs, preferences, market structure, and the welfare measure. The result is conditional and does not establish that opaque decisions are generally desirable.

## Consumer Agency

The analysis makes a trade-off explicit. A conditional welfare result must not be used to dismiss requirements for explanation or meaningful appeal.

Assessment: protective. Mechanism features: opacity_disclosure, delegation_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
