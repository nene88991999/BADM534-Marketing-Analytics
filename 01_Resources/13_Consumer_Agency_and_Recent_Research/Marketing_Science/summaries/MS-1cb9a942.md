# When Bulldozers Loom: Informal Property Rights and Marketing Practice Innovation Among Emerging Market Microentrepreneurs

Magda Hassan; Jaideep Prabhu; Rajesh Chandy; Om Narasimhan. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.1379).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2023-01.

## Managerial Implications

Informal leasing can provide a more secure basis for investment than possession without ownership in the studied Cairo market. Property-right uncertainty can suppress marketing innovation. Programs supporting microentrepreneurs should consider asset security alongside skills and credit.

## Consumer Agency

The research identifies institutional constraints on entrepreneurial action rather than a consumer persuasion tactic.
