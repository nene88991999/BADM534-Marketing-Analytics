# Frontiers: Breaking the Glass Ceiling: Empowering Female Entrepreneurs Through Female Mentors

Frank Germann; Stephen J. Anderson; Pradeep K. Chintagunta; Naufel Vilcassim. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0108).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2024-01.

## Managerial Implications

A randomized mentoring study in Uganda finds substantial gains for women entrepreneurs paired with women mentors. Program managers should consider mentor matching and the quality of engagement, while evaluating results in their own institutional setting.

## Consumer Agency

The intervention expands entrepreneurial capability and opportunity.
