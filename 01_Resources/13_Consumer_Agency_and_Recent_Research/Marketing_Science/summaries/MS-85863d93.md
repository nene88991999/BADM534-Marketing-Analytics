# Bad Apples on Rotten Tomatoes: Critics, Crowds, and Gender Bias in Product Ratings

Luis Aguiar. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0236).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2026-01.

## Managerial Implications

Crowd ratings can contain selection-driven gender bias relative to professional reviews. Platform design affects which evaluations become visible and consequential. Managers should assess reviewer composition and rating-distribution changes before presenting scores as neutral quality signals.

## Consumer Agency

Apparently neutral crowd scores may transmit biased judgments. Assess participation and display design and permit informed comparison.
