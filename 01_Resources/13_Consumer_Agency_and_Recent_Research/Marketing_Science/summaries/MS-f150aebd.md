## Managerial Implications

Privacy policy can create benefits alongside effects on competition, innovation, and underserved customers. The review emphasizes heterogeneous preferences and firm capabilities. Evaluate distributional consequences rather than assume either more data collection or more restriction uniformly improves welfare.

## Consumer Agency
