# AI in Disguise—Quasi-Experimental Analysis of a Large-Scale Deployment of AI-Generated Ads

Yannick Exner; Jochen Hartmann; Ziqian Ding; Shunyuan Zhang; Oded Netzer. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2025.0547).

Online publication: 2026-10-19. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

AI-generated ads have heterogeneous performance depending partly on whether they appear AI-generated. The quasi-experimental comparison does not detect an average click-rate disadvantage, which does not prove equivalence. Evaluate accuracy, provenance, and downstream outcomes alongside clicks.

## Consumer Agency

Realistic synthetic imagery can obscure provenance or misrepresent experience. Verify substantive claims and disclose origin where consequential.
