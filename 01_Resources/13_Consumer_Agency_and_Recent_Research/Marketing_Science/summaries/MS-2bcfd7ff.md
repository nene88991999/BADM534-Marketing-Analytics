# Frontiers: The Effect of an Ad Ban on Retailer Sales: Insights from a Natural Experiment

Sebastian Gabel; Dominik Molitor; Martin Spann. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0019).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2024-07.

## Managerial Implications

A temporary grocery print-advertising ban reduced the studied retailer's sales while leaving aggregate market demand broadly unchanged. Advertising can therefore redistribute purchases across retailers. Regulatory and budget decisions should separate firm-level effects from category demand.

## Consumer Agency

The evidence evaluates an advertising restriction and substitution, without establishing consumer agency erosion.
