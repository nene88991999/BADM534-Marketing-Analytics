# Frontiers: The Interplay of User-Generated Content, Content Industry Revenues, and Platform Regulation: Quasi-Experimental Evidence from YouTube

Nils Wlömert; Dominik Papies; Michel Clement; Martin Spann. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.0080).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2024-01.

## Managerial Implications

User-generated music availability can stimulate demand for many songs while cannibalizing prominent recent releases. Aggregate revenue effects can differ from the typical song's outcome. Licensing and platform decisions should account for this distribution rather than apply a uniform policy.
