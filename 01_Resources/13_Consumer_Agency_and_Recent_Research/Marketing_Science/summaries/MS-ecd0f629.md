# Choice Architecture, Privacy Valuations, and Selection Bias in Consumer Data

## Managerial Implications

Consent defaults and price anchors change elicited privacy valuations and which consumers enter a dataset. Maximizing consent volume can improve or worsen representativeness depending on heterogeneity. Data teams should evaluate coverage and selection alongside participation rates.

## Consumer Agency

Consent optimization can change the measured preference it purports to reveal. Use understandable choices and test active-choice benchmarks.
