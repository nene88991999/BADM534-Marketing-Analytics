# Injunctive Norms Outperform Reciprocity: Tip Request Messages Can Increase Tipping Without Reducing Demand

Seung Hyun Kim; On Amir; Kenneth C. Wilbur. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2025.0392).

Online publication: 2026-10-30. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Tip-request language changes tipping in field experiments, with effects varying across app and web channels. Managers should evaluate worker compensation, customer spending, and later demand. A demand response does not reveal whether customers felt pressured.

## Consumer Agency

Norm messages can turn a voluntary payment into perceived obligation. Keep declining easy and distinguish tips from required charges.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: social_cues, financial_commitment. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
