# Targeted Advertising as Implicit Recommendation: Strategic Mistargeting and Personal Data Opt-out

Z. Eddie Ning; Jiwoong Shin; Jungju Yu. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0117).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2025-03.

## Managerial Implications

Targeted ads can act as implicit recommendations, giving firms incentives to contact poorly matched customers. Pricing and privacy defaults change the equilibrium. Evaluate recommendation credibility and consumer surplus alongside targeting accuracy.

## Consumer Agency

Accurate prediction can coexist with misaligned targeting incentives. Disclose sponsorship and provide usable privacy choices.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: personalization, privacy_control, opacity_disclosure. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
