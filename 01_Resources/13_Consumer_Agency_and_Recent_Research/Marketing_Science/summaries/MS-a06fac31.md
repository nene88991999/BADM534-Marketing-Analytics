# How Does Prepopulating Search Bars with Keywords Affect Online Consumer Behavior? A Field Experiment

Chenshuo Sun. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2024.0793).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2025-11.

## Managerial Implications

Prepopulated search terms change product discovery and purchase behavior. Personalized suggestions can reduce self-entered queries even while raising conversion. Platforms should measure user-directed search and match quality alongside sales.

## Consumer Agency

Suggested terms can narrow the search a customer would otherwise formulate. Make replacement and independent search easy.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: defaults_order, personalization, delegation_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
