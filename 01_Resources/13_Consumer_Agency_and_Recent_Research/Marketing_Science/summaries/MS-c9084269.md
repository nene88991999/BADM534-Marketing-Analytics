# Banning Unsolicited Store Flyers: Does Helping the Environment Hurt Retailing?

Jonne Y. Guyt; Arjen van Lin; Kristopher O. Keller. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0020).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2025-10.

## Managerial Implications

Switching unsolicited flyers to opt-in substantially reduced distribution in the studied Dutch municipalities with little measured change in grocery shopping. Managers should measure information use and substitution when evaluating reduced unsolicited communication.

## Consumer Agency

Requiring an affirmative request can restore control over unsolicited messages. The observed shopping response is setting-specific.

Assessment: protective. Mechanism features: defaults_order. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
