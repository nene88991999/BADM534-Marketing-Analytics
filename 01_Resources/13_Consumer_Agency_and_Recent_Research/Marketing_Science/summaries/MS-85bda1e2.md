# Don’t Hurry, Be Happy! The Bright Side of Late Product Release

Mushegh Harutyunyan; Chakravarthi Narasimhan. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0393).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2024-11.

## Managerial Implications

A late product launch can relax price competition enough to offset lost early sales under the model's growth and differentiation conditions. Timing decisions should evaluate forward-looking demand and competitive response. This is a conditional equilibrium result rather than a universal recommendation to delay.

## Consumer Agency

Strategic launch timing affects market outcomes, but the model does not by itself establish impaired individual agency.
