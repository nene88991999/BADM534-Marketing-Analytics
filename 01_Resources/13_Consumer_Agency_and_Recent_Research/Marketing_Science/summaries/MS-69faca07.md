# Job Security, Gender, and Sales Performance: Evidence from a Retail Sales Context

Diego F. Salazar; Noah Lim; Ingrid Koch. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0233).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2026-01.

## Managerial Implications

Permanent employment is associated with higher sales productivity in the studied Indonesian retailer. The analysis examines gender and contextual heterogeneity. Managers should evaluate job stability as part of work design rather than assume insecurity improves effort.

## Consumer Agency

Greater employment security can support workers' capacity to act and participate.
