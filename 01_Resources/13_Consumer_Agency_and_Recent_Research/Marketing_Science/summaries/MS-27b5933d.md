# Applying Large Language Models to Sponsored Search Advertising

Martin Reisenbichler; Thomas Reutterer; David A. Schweidel. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0611).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2026-01.

## Managerial Implications

A human-supervised LLM application improves search-ad performance in two experimental settings, with gains depending on budgets and landing-page conditions. Managers should evaluate the complete workflow and costs rather than assume an untailored model improves every campaign.

## Consumer Agency

Automated message optimization can scale misleading emphasis if accuracy and comprehension are absent from the objective. Review claims and customer understanding.
