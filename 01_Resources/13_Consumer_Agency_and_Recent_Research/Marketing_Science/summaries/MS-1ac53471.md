# Close Enough? A Large-Scale Exploration of Non-Experimental Approaches to Advertising Measurement

Brett R. Gordon; Robert Moakler; Florian Zettelmeyer. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.1413).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2023-07.

## Managerial Implications

Rich observational data and flexible machine learning can still fail to recover experimental advertising effects. The comparison with many randomized studies cautions against treating adjusted associations as causal returns. Use experiments or explicitly justified identification conditions when allocating advertising budgets.
