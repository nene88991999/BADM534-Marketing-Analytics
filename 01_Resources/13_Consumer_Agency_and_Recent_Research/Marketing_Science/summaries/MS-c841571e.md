# Advertising Platforms and Privacy

Ruizhi Zhu; Sridhar Moorthy; Xianwen Shi. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2024.0879).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2025-11.

## Managerial Implications

Privacy restrictions can change product competition and advertising prices, so consumers may value privacy for instrumental economic reasons. Welfare results depend on platform market power and cross-product substitution. A complete evaluation should also state which intrinsic privacy interests the model omits.

## Consumer Agency

The model identifies economic consequences of targeting restrictions without equating modeled surplus with all dimensions of autonomy.

Assessment: protective. Mechanism features: privacy_control, personalization. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
