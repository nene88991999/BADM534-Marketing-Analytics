# Frontiers: Framing Price Increase as Discount: A New Manipulation of Reference Price

Sungsik Park; Man Xie; Jinhong Xie. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.1402).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2023-01.

## Managerial Implications

A seller can make a price increase appear to be a discount by introducing a list price at the same time. The study documents this practice across tracked online categories. Evaluate actual price histories and consumer understanding rather than the displayed discount alone.

## Consumer Agency

A reference-price display can reverse the apparent direction of a real price change. Preserve accessible price history and truthful savings claims.

Assessment: reported choice concern. Mechanism features: framing_reference, opacity_disclosure. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
