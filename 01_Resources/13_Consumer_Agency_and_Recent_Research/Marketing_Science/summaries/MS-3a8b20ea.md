# Designing Loot Boxes: Implications for Profits and Welfare

Jin Miao; Sanjay Jain. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0007).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2024-11.

## Managerial Implications

A stylized loot-box model allows profit and total-welfare gains through market expansion, while some guaranteed-acquisition designs reduce consumer welfare. Managers should separate total surplus, consumer surplus, spending risk, and assumptions about buyer behavior.

## Consumer Agency

Repeated probabilistic purchases can create escalating spending. Transparent odds, cumulative-cost information, and meaningful limits support control.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: habits_rewards, financial_commitment. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
