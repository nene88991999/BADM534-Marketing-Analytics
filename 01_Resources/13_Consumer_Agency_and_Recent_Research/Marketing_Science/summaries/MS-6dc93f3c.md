# Choice Frictions in Large Assortments

Olivia R. Natan. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2023.0415).

## Managerial Implications

Assortment expansion can attract customers while reducing purchase frequency among existing customers. The available dissertation chapter models limited information and choice frictions. Managers should evaluate search burden, acquisition, and repeat use together when changing assortment size.

## Consumer Agency

Complexity can make meaningful comparison costly. Provide useful filters and preserve access to the full choice set.
