# Estimating Parameters of Structural Models Using Neural Networks

Yanhao (Max) Wei; Zhenling Jiang. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.0360).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2025-01.

## Managerial Implications

Neural networks trained on simulated datasets can estimate parameters of structural models with high simulation costs. Computational efficiency depends on the model and training support. Analysts should validate parameter recovery and uncertainty before interpreting counterfactual results.
