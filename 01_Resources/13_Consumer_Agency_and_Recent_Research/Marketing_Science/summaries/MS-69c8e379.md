# Frontiers: Polarized America: From Political Polarization to Preference Polarization

Verena Schoenmueller; Oded Netzer; Florian Stahl. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.1408).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2023-01.

## Managerial Implications

Political polarization can extend to brand preferences and purchases. Brands taking a stance may change the composition of their customer base. Managers should measure these shifts across behavior and stated preference without assuming political identity determines every individual decision.

## Consumer Agency

Political-affinity profiles can be used to substitute identity cues for product evaluation. Limit profiling and provide substantive information.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: emotion_identity, personalization. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
