# The Effects of Search Interface on Consumer Preferences: Evidence from a Randomized Experiment

Jia Liu; Olivier Toubia. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2025.0261).

Online publication: 2026-10-21. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Search filters change exploration and measured preferences, not merely transaction speed. A randomized interface experiment finds changes in prices paid, attribute weights, and market concentration. Evaluate navigation and preference formation alongside conversion.

## Consumer Agency

Interface restrictions can shape which attributes people learn to value. Preserve useful filtering and opportunities to reconsider.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: exit_search_friction, delegation_control, attention_salience. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
