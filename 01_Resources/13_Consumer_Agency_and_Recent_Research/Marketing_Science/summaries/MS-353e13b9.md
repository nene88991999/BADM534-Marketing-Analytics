# Smart Green Nudging: Reducing Product Returns Through Digital Footprints and Causal Machine Learning

Moritz von Zahn; Kevin Bauer; Cristina Mihale-Wilson; Johanna Jagow; Maximilian Speicher; Oliver Hinz. Marketing Science. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mksc.2022.0393).

Online publication: not recorded. Issue publication: 2025-07.

## Managerial Implications

A randomized field experiment finds that environmental information can reduce apparel returns, with causal-model personalization offering additional potential gains. Managers should distinguish the randomized average effect from off-policy estimates and ensure the intervention does not obstruct warranted returns.

## Consumer Agency

Environmental appeals can pressure customers to retain unsuitable goods. Preserve return rights and measure regret or unresolved product problems.
