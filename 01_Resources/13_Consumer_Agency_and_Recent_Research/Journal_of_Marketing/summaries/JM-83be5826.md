# Business-to-Investor Marketing: The Interplay of Costly and Costless Signals

Greg Nyilasy; Shangwen Yi; Dennis Herhausen; Stephan Ludwig; Darren W. Dahl. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429241288464).

Online publication: 2024-12-18. Issue publication: 2025-05.

## Managerial Implications

Startup pitches benefit from matching language to verifiable resource signals. Passion can help when substantive endowments are present but can backfire when they are absent. Concreteness also has contingent effects. Investors should evaluate supporting evidence separately from rhetorical confidence.

## Consumer Agency

Pitch language can shift judgments under uncertainty. Verify the substantive resource signals on which the claimed credibility depends.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: emotion_identity, framing_reference. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
