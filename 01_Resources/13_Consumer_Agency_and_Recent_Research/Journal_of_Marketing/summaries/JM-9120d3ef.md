# Complaint De-Escalation Strategies on Social Media

Dennis Herhausen; Lauren Grewal; Krista Hill Cummings; Anne L. Roggeveen; Francisco Villarroel Ordenes; Dhruv Grewal. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429221119977).

Online publication: 2022-11-16. Issue publication: 2023-03.

## Managerial Implications

Active listening and empathy can reduce negative arousal in public social-media complaints, even before the underlying service failure is resolved. Service teams should pair responsive language with actual remediation and track resolution separately from the customer's immediate emotional response.

## Consumer Agency

Emotional calming could substitute for redress if the firm treats reduced complaint intensity as resolution. Track remedial action and preserve escalation options.
