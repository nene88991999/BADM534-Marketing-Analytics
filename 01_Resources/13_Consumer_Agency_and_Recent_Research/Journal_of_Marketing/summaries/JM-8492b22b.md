# Curating the Crowd: How Firms Manage Social Fit to Stage Social Atmospheres

Ilias Danatzis; Tim Hill; Ingo O. Karpen; Michael Kleinaltenkamp. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251328277).

Online publication: 2025-06-20. Issue publication: 2026-03.

## Managerial Implications

Experience businesses shape social atmosphere through customer selection and the management of shared meanings. Ethnographic evidence shows how these practices influence fit and belonging. Managers should assess inclusion and exclusion alongside loyalty and experiential value.

## Consumer Agency

Belonging and selective admission can create pressure to conform or conceal exclusion. Use transparent access criteria and accessible alternatives.
