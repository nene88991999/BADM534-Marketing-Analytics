# Increasing Accountability and Compliance with Robot Advice

Jana Holthöwer; Jenny van Doorn; Stephanie M. Noble. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251370268).

Online publication: 2025-08-08. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Advice from a robot receives greater compliance when attributed to a human source, through perceived accountability. Social cues can change this response. Evaluate appropriateness and understanding of the advice, and preserve refusal and escalation.

## Consumer Agency

Perceived obligation to justify refusal can pressure adherence. Ensure advice is warranted and declining it carries no hidden penalty.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: social_cues, delegation_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
