# EXPRESS: Channel Adoption Pathways and Post-Adoption Behavior

Shirsho Biswas; Hema Yoganarasimhan; Haonan Zhang. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429261478875).

Online publication: 2026-08-09. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Customers' paths into online shopping predict different later spending and channel use. Promotion-induced adoption can involve forward buying and weaker subsequent profitability. Managers should incorporate adoption history when forecasting customer value and promotion returns.
