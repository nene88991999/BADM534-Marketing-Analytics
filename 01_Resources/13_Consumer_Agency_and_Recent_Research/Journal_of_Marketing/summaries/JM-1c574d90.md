# Conceptual Contributions in Marketing Scholarship: Patterns, Mechanisms, and Rebalancing Options

Bastian Kindermann; Daniel Wentzel; David Antons; Torsten-Oliver Salge. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429231196122).

Online publication: 2023-11-19. Issue publication: 2024-05.

## Managerial Implications

Marketing research has increasingly favored introducing and explaining concepts over connecting or debating them. Research managers and editors can broaden evaluation criteria to support useful synthesis and critical comparison. The evidence combines a large article corpus with qualitative interviews.
