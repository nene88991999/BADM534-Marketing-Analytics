# Lower Artificial Intelligence Literacy Predicts Greater AI Receptivity

Stephanie M. Tully; Chiara Longoni; Gil Appel. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251314491).

Online publication: 2025-04-21. Issue publication: 2025-09.

## Managerial Implications

Lower AI literacy is associated with greater receptivity partly through perceptions of magic and awe. Adoption metrics may therefore reflect misunderstanding. Managers should evaluate informed adoption and provide understandable explanations of system capabilities and limits.

## Consumer Agency

Targeting misunderstanding can produce excessive trust. Calibrated explanations and meaningful human alternatives protect choice.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: emotion_identity, delegation_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
