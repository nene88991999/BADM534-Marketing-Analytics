# The Enrichment Economy: Market Dynamics, Brand Strategy, and Ethics

Delphine Dion; Roman Pavlyuchenko; and Sonja Prokopec. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429241275014).

Online publication: 2024-11-26. Issue publication: 2025-03.

## Managerial Implications

Luxury brands operate across primary sales and resale markets in which consumers seek capital gains. Portfolio and relationship decisions should account for resale incentives, market access, and unequal exposure to risk. Ethnographic evidence helps explain how brands sustain these connected markets.

## Consumer Agency

Investment and status narratives may encourage risky acquisition when resale gains are uncertain. Disclose downside risk and distinguish use value from expected appreciation.
