# Targeting Nearby Influencers: The Acceleration of Natural Triadic Closure by Leveraging Interconnectors

Jacob Goldenberg; Andreas Lanz; Daniel Shapira; Florian Stahl. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429231223420).

Online publication: 2024-02-26. Issue publication: 2024-09.

## Managerial Implications

Connected followers can help a creator reach nearby users more effectively than seeding disconnected high-status influencers. The evidence combines observational studies and a field experiment. Managers should evaluate network position and actual incremental reach, alongside audience size.

## Consumer Agency

Network proximity may amplify perceived peer endorsement. Make coordinated commercial promotion identifiable and retain user controls over recommendations.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: social_cues, personalization. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
