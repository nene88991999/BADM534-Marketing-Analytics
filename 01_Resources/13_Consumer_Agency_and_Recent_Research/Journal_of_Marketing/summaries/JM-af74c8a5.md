# Who We Are and How We Govern: The Effect of Identity Orientation on Governance Choice

Jan B. Heide; Simon J. Bell; Paul Tracey. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429221094027).

Online publication: 2022-05-22. Issue publication: 2023-01.

## Managerial Implications

Governance arrangements should reflect both partners' organizational identities and their interdependence. Apparently compatible identities can conceal different expectations. Managers should diagnose these differences before selecting contractual and relational governance practices.

## Consumer Agency

The conceptual framework addresses organizational governance rather than individual consumer choice.
