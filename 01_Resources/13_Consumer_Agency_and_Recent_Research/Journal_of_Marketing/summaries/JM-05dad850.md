# Buy Now, Pay Later: Impact of Installment Payments on Customer Purchases

Stijn Maesen; Dionysius Ang. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429241282414).

Online publication: 2024-12-05. Issue publication: 2025-05.

## Managerial Implications

Installment payments can increase purchase incidence and spending by reducing perceived financial constraints. Evaluate affordability across all commitments and subsequent repayment outcomes alongside retailer sales. The paper combines transaction analysis with experiments on perceived costs.

## Consumer Agency

Smaller installment amounts can obscure the total obligation. Display total cost and cumulative commitments prominently.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: framing_reference, financial_commitment. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
