# Meaning of Manual Labor Impedes Consumer Adoption of Autonomous Products

Emanuel de Bellis; Gita Venkataramani Johar; Nicola Poletti. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429231171841).

Online publication: 2023-06-15. Issue publication: 2023-11.

## Managerial Implications

Customers who derive meaning from manual tasks may resist autonomous products. Communicating meaningful alternative uses of saved time can improve acceptance. Product teams should also preserve valued manual participation rather than assuming every task is an unwanted burden.

## Consumer Agency

Meaning-based appeals could override a preference to retain control. Offer genuine manual and automated modes and explain their consequences.
