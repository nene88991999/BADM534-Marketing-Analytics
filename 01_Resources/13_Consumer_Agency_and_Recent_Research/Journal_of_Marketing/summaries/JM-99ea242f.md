# Authenticity in Influencer Marketing: How Can Influencers and Brands Work Together to Build and Maintain Influencer Authenticity?

Barbara Duffek; Andreas B. Eisingerich; Omar Merlo; Guan Lee. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251319786).

Online publication: 2025-05-05. Issue publication: 2025-09.

## Managerial Implications

Influencer authenticity depends on how consumers, creators, brands, and agencies interpret the relationship. These expectations can conflict. Partnership management should make creative autonomy, brand fit, and commercial expectations explicit rather than treating authenticity as a single score.

## Consumer Agency

Apparent authenticity can obscure commercial direction. Sponsorship transparency and truthful product accounts preserve informed evaluation.
