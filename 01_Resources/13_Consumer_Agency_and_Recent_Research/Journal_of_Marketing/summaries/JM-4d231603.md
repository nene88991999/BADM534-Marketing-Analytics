# Managing Brand Relationship Plurality: Insights from the Nonprofit Sector

Verena Gruber; Jonathan Deschênes. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429241253193).

Online publication: 2024-07-23. Issue publication: 2024-11.

## Managerial Implications

Nonprofits can sustain episodic volunteer relationships without continuously intensifying commitment. Distinct recruitment and activation practices can coexist with deeper relationship development. Managers should offer participation forms that match volunteers' desired involvement.

## Consumer Agency

Respecting non-escalating participation supports a person's control over the intensity of involvement.
