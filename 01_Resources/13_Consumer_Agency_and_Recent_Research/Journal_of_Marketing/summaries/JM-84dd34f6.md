# Preference Filtering: When Customers Share Narrow Preferences with Algorithms

Phyliss Jia Gai; Eugina Leung; Anne-Kathrin Klesse. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429261466254).

Online publication: 2026-06-25. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

People may report narrower preferences to algorithms because they expect uniform weighting. This can reduce recommendation fit despite appearing rational beforehand. Preference elicitation should explain weighting and permit nuanced revisions.

## Consumer Agency

An opaque elicitation process can narrow the interests the system recognizes. Enable weighting, correction, and exploration outside the inferred profile.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: personalization, delegation_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
