# Customer Data Privacy Stewardship

Jordan W. Moffett; Natalie Chisam; Kelly D. Martin; Robert W. Palmatier. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251367342).

Online publication: 2026-01-12. Issue publication: 2026-11.

## Managerial Implications

Responsible privacy management can contribute to customer and firm performance, depending on perceived motives and data risk. Privacy should be assessed as a service commitment with verifiable practices rather than solely as compliance messaging.

## Consumer Agency

Customer control and credible stewardship can support informed data decisions.
