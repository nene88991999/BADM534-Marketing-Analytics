# The Impact of Organic Specialist Store Entry on Category Performance at Incumbent Stores

Stijn Maesen; Lien Lamey. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429221090983).

Online publication: 2022-05-22. Issue publication: 2023-01.

## Managerial Implications

Generalist grocery stores face sales losses after a nearby organic specialist enters. A broader organic assortment, credible specialist brands, and organic feature advertising can improve resilience; frequent deep organic discounts appear less protective. The evidence concerns category performance around observed store entry.
