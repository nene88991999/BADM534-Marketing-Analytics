# How E-Scooters Impact Shared Mobility and Consumer Safety

Ruichun Liu; Unnati Narang. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251393987).

Online publication: 2026-03-07. Issue publication: 2026-09.

## Managerial Implications

E-scooter entry in the Chicago setting changes demand for other mobility services and is associated with safety and environmental consequences. Managers and policymakers should evaluate substitution and neighborhood heterogeneity alongside operator revenue. The design uses generalized synthetic controls.
