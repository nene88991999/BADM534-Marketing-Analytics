# Standing Out While Fitting In: Visual Design of Text Overlays in Social Media Communication

Stefania Farace; Francisco Villarroel Ordenes; Dennis Herhausen; Dhruv Grewal; Ko de Ruyter. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251322773).

Online publication: 2025-05-29. Issue publication: 2026-01.

## Managerial Implications

Text overlays interact with the underlying image. Large central text combined with dynamic imagery can reduce visual appeal and engagement. Managers should evaluate the complete composition rather than optimize text size, position, and motion separately.

## Consumer Agency

Attention-capturing layout may make promotional claims more visible than material conditions. Give consequential terms adequate visibility.
