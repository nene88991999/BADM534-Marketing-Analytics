# Learning from Data: An Empirics-First Approach to Relevant Knowledge Generation

Peter N. Golder; Marnik G. Dekimpe; Jake T. An; Harald J. van Heerde; Darren S.U. Kim; Joseph W. Alba. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429221129200).

Online publication: 2022-12-29. Issue publication: 2023-05.

## Managerial Implications

Research teams can begin with a concrete market phenomenon and purposeful empirical exploration before developing theory. The practical requirement is disciplined measurement and transparent reporting of how patterns were discovered and validated, including the distinction between exploration and confirmation.
