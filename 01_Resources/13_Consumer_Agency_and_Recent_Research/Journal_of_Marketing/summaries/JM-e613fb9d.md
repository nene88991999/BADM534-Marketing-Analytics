# Emotional Energy: When Customer Interactions Energize Service Employees

Julien Cayla; Brigitte Auriacombe. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429241260637).

Online publication: 2024-08-08. Issue publication: 2025-01.

## Managerial Implications

Customer interactions can regenerate employees' emotional energy when shared attention, mood, autonomy, and status support the encounter. Service managers should consider these features of work design alongside demands for emotional labor.

## Consumer Agency
