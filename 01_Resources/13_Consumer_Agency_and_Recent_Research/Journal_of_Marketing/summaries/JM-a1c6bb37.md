# Too Many or Too Few? Information Cues in Recommender Systems and Consequences for Search and Purchase Behavior

Xing Fang; SunAh Kim; Pradeep K. Chintagunta. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251326941).

Online publication: 2025-06-27. Issue publication: 2026-01.

## Managerial Implications

Recommendation information affects both search intensity and efficiency. A field experiment finds the highest sales with one cue, while richer information reduces further exploration. Evaluate match quality and informed comparison alongside conversion.

## Consumer Agency

Withholding useful comparison information can increase search or sales while making evaluation harder. Keep material attributes accessible.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: attention_salience, opacity_disclosure, exit_search_friction. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
