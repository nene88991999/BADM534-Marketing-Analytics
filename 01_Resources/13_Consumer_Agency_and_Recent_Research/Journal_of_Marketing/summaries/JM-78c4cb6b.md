# Whispers in Your Mind: The Role of Voice Features in Customer Acquisition and Retention

Haifeng Xu; Yi Ding; Yu Ding; Qi Zhang; Cheng Zhang. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251328293).

Online publication: 2025-06-12. Issue publication: 2026-01.

## Managerial Implications

Sales-call voice features have different associations with acquisition and retention. The studies identify arousal and perceived competence as relevant mechanisms, while finding no purchase-volume effect. Sales evaluation should distinguish conversion, timing, and purchase volume.

## Consumer Agency

Vocal arousal and apparent competence can influence trust independently of product quality. Preserve factual explanations and unpressured refusal.
