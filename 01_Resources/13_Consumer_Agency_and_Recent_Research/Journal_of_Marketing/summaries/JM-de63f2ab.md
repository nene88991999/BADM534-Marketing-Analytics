# Competition and the Regulation of Fictitious Pricing

Richard Staelin; Joel E. Urbany; Donald Ngwe. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429231164640).

Online publication: 2023-08-01. Issue publication: 2023-11.

## Managerial Implications

Competition can sustain fictitious comparison prices rather than eliminate them. The article examines disclosure of actual prior prices as a possible remedy. Managers should ground advertised savings in verifiable prices and evaluate whether customers understand the comparison.

## Consumer Agency

Inflated reference prices can distort deal evaluation. Verifiable price history can support informed comparison.

Assessment: reported choice concern. Mechanism features: framing_reference, opacity_disclosure. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
