# How High-Arousal Language Shapes Micro- Versus Macro-Influencers’ Impact

Giovanni Luca Cascio Rizzo; Francisco Villarroel Ordenes; Rumen Pozharliev; Matteo De Angelis; Michele Costabile. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429231207636).

Online publication: 2024-01-08. Issue publication: 2024-07.

## Managerial Implications

High-arousal language increases engagement for micro-influencers but can reduce it for macro-influencers through perceived trustworthiness. Balanced assessments and informative intent can change the response. Campaign evaluation should account for communicator scale and credibility, alongside engagement.

## Consumer Agency

Arousal and apparent informativity can alter trust without improving evidence about the product. Disclose commercial relationships and verify claims.
