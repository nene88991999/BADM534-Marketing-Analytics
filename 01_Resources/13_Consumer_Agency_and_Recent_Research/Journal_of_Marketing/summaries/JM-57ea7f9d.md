# Consequences of Marketing Asset Accountability—A Natural Experiment

Peter Guenther; Miriam Guenther; Bryan A. Lukas; Christian Homburg. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429241236142).

Online publication: 2024-04-22. Issue publication: 2024-09.

## Managerial Implications

A change in Australian accounting rules provides evidence that marketing-asset accountability can improve long-term marketing efficiency and stock-price informativeness while reducing equity costs. Short-term efficiency and debt-cost effects are less consistent. Financial reporting consequences should be evaluated separately across these outcomes.
