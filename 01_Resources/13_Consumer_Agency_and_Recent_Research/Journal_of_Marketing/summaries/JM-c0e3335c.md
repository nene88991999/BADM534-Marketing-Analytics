# Leveraging Rational Addiction Theory to Reduce Mobile Usage

Jeeva Somasundaram; Laura Zimmermann; Quang Duc Pham. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251405841).

Online publication: 2025-12-02. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Preannounced fixed usage-reduction targets can induce earlier reductions in smartphone use. The trials support a forward-looking habit mechanism with heterogeneous responses. Digital-well-being programs should evaluate sustained usage changes and voluntary goal alignment.

## Consumer Agency

Habit-based intervention can strengthen self-directed control. The outcome and the customer's chosen goal determine the welfare interpretation.

Assessment: protective. Mechanism features: habits_rewards, financial_commitment. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
