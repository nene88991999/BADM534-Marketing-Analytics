# Closing the Knowledge Gap: Understanding and Reducing the Environmental Impact of Food Choices

Bart J. Bronnenberg; Trang Bùi; Barbara Deleersnyder; Lesley Haerkens; George Knox; Arjen van Lin; Max J. Pachali; Anna Paley; Robert W. Smith; Samuel Stäbler. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251348436).

Online publication: 2025-08-28. Issue publication: 2026-09.

## Managerial Implications

Personalized environmental feedback can improve food-impact knowledge and shift stated choices toward lower emissions. Peer comparisons and category information contribute differently. Managers should track knowledge retention and actual behavior separately from stated choices.

## Consumer Agency

The intervention supplies information aligned with a stated sustainability objective. Peer comparison and personalization are dual-use mechanisms whose welfare direction depends on implementation.
