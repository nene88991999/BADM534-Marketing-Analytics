# A War on Sugar? Effects of Reduced Sugar Content and Package Size in the Soda Category

Kristopher O. Keller; Jonne Y. Guyt. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429231152181).

Online publication: 2023-05-10. Issue publication: 2023-09.

## Managerial Implications

Soda brands can sometimes reduce sugar while protecting sales. Smaller package introductions perform well on average, while sugar reformulation depends on labeling, branding, and packaging. Evaluate sugar purchased at the category level alongside product sales before describing a health benefit.

## Consumer Agency

The evidence supports evaluating product and public-health outcomes together; it does not itself establish loss of agency.
