# Free Riding on Product Returns to Drive Profits

Elisa Montaguti; Scott Neslin; Sara Valentini. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251409673).

Online publication: 2025-12-11. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Returns can influence both future purchases and future returns. Retailers should evaluate these dynamics jointly when choosing marketing spending and return policies. A strict policy that minimizes immediate returns can sacrifice valuable longer-term customer activity.
