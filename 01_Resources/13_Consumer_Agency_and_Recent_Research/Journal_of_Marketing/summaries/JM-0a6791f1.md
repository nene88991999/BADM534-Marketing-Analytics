# Immersive Service: Characteristics, Challenges, and Pathways to Consumer Agency

Laurel Anderson; Catharina von Koskull; Martin Mende; Johanna Gummerus. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251319312).

Online publication: 2025-05-16. Issue publication: 2025-09.

## Managerial Implications

Services that surround a person's daily life, including residential care, can constrain control through boundaries, roles, competing voices, and protocols. Managers should support residents' voice, meaningful responsibilities, and opportunities to challenge routines. The evidence is an ethnographic account of agency in immersive service.

## Consumer Agency

Institutional routines and dependence can narrow practical choices even when formal choices remain. Provide effective voice, review, and independent assistance.
