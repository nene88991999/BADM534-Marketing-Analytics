# Beyond Opt-Out: How Presumed-Consent Language Shapes Persuasion

Romain Cadario; Jenny Zimmermann; Bram Van den Bergh. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251323885).

Online publication: 2025-06-05. Issue publication: 2026-01.

## Managerial Implications

Presumed-consent wording can increase behavior even when the formal policy remains opt-in. Endorsement and ownership inferences help explain the response across settings. Evaluate what customers believe has been decided, alongside the legal form of consent.

## Consumer Agency

Language can imply a decision has already been made. Clearly state the person's ability to decline and avoid fabricated commitments.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: defaults_order, social_cues, framing_reference. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
