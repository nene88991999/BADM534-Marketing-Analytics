# Human or AI? Identity Salience Enhances Historically Marginalized (but Not Privileged) Consumers’ Choice of Algorithmic Service Providers

Yeseul Kim; Dipayan Biswas; Courtney Szocs; Khalia Jenkins. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429261444284).

Online publication: 2026-04-08. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Historically marginalized customers may prefer algorithmic service when identity-based judgment is salient. Automated options can improve access in some nondiverse service settings. Managers should address the source of exclusion while retaining genuine service-channel choice.

## Consumer Agency

Choice among service channels can protect agency. Steering vulnerable customers into an opaque compulsory channel would reverse that benefit.

Assessment: protective. Mechanism features: emotion_identity, delegation_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
