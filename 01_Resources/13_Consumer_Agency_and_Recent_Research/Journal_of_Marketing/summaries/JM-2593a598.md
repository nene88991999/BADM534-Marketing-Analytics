# A Practical Guide to Endogeneity Correction Using Copulas

Yi Qian; Anthony Koschmann; Hui Xie. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251410844).

Online publication: 2026-05-20. Issue publication: 2026-07.

## Managerial Implications

Copula approaches can address certain endogeneity problems without conventional instruments, but their validity depends on data and identification conditions. Analysts should check those conditions and model specifications before interpreting adjusted coefficients causally.
