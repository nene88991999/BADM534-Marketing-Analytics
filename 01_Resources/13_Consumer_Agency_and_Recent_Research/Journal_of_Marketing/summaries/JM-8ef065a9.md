# Why Is It Wrong to Sell Your Body? Understanding Liberals’ Versus Conservatives’ Moral Objections to Bodily Markets

Shreyans Goenka; Stijn M.J. van Osselaer. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429211046936).

Online publication: 2021-11-17. Issue publication: 2023-01.

## Managerial Implications

Moral objections to bodily markets differ by political orientation: exploitation concerns are more salient for liberals, while sanctity concerns are more salient for conservatives. Advocacy and policy communications should address these distinct concerns and distinguish living from deceased bodily products.

## Consumer Agency

Values-based messages could pressure people when their moral identities are used without balanced information. Preserve deliberation and explain competing considerations.
