# When and Why Consumers React Negatively to Brand Acquisitions: A Values Authenticity Account

Alessandro Biraglia; Christoph Fuchs; Elisa Maira; Stefano Puntoni. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429221137817).

Online publication: 2023-03-17. Issue publication: 2023-07.

## Managerial Implications

Acquisitions can damage an acquired brand when customers perceive a loss of its distinctive values. Managers should evaluate value alignment, leadership continuity, brand age, and acquisition communication when assessing integration decisions. The studies support a perceived-authenticity explanation.

## Consumer Agency

Authenticity language could conceal substantive changes after an acquisition. Communicate actual continuity and change rather than using identity reassurance alone.
