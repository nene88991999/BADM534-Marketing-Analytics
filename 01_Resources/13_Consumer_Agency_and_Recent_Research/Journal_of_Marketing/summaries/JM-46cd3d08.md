# Choice Architecture for Healthier Insurance Decisions: Ordering and Partitioning Together Can Improve Consumer Choice

Benedict G.C. Dellaert; Eric J. Johnson; Shannon Duncan; Tom Baker. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429221119086).

Online publication: 2022-11-13. Issue publication: 2024-01.

## Managerial Implications

Ordering and partitioning insurance options can jointly improve choices when the ordering reflects a reliable consumer model. The same partition can impair discovery if better options sit lower in the list. Validate the ranking objective and preserve access to alternatives.

## Consumer Agency

Partitioning can restrict effective search without removing formal options. Test customer-relevant ranking quality and the ease of exploring alternatives.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: defaults_order, attention_salience, exit_search_friction. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
