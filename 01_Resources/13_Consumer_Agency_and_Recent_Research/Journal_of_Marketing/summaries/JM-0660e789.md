## Managerial Implications

Rural cardiology access depends on physician travel costs, competition, and patient demand. A structural model compares targeted recruitment with subsidies under a physician-supply reduction. Managers can use such allocation models to identify locations at greatest risk, subject to the model's behavioral assumptions.
