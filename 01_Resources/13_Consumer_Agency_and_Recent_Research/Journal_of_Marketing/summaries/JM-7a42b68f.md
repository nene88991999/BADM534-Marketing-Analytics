# DICE: Advancing Social Media Research Through Digital In-Context Experiments

Hauke Roggenkamp; Johannes Boegershausen; Christian Hildebrand. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251371702).

Online publication: 2026-02-27. Issue publication: 2026-09.

## Managerial Implications

Experiments using entire scrollable feeds can measure contextual spillovers and competition for attention that isolated-post studies miss. Researchers should measure scrolling and recall within the relevant environment and distinguish experimental control from external validity.

## Consumer Agency

Feed optimization can prioritize sustained attention over users' intentions. Include comprehension, stopping, and user-selected limits in evaluation.
