# Cushioning the Blow: Reducing Customer Attrition in Response to Price Increase Notifications

Hoorsana Damavandi; Kersi D. Antia; Praveen K. Kopalle. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429261451752).

Online publication: 2026-05-11. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Explanations for price increases affect attrition and perceived switching costs. The storage-provider studies find different responses to market, cost, and quality explanations. Communicate truthful reasons while preserving customers' ability to assess outside options.

## Consumer Agency

Messages can discourage exit by altering beliefs about alternatives. Provide accurate comparisons and straightforward cancellation.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: framing_reference, exit_search_friction. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
