# Responsible Design Thinking

Minu Kumar; Pietro Micheli; Jatinder Jit (J.J.) Singh; Neil Goldberg. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251396423).

Online publication: 2025-11-01. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Design teams can overlook affected stakeholders, environmental consequences, and harms that emerge during rapid prototyping or scaling. Responsible design requires broader inclusion, anticipation of consequences, and governance. Interview evidence motivates these organizational capabilities.
