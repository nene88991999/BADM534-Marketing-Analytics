# Innovation Imprinting: Why Some Firms Beat the Post-IPO Innovation Slump

Simone Wies; Christine Moorman; Rajesh K. Chandy. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429221114317).

Online publication: 2022-08-08. Issue publication: 2023-03.

## Managerial Implications

Firms that establish a record of breakthrough innovation before an IPO may attract investors willing to support continued innovation. Managers should align the innovation strategy and investor base before listing. Evidence from consumer packaged goods firms supports this explanation of heterogeneous post-IPO innovation trajectories.
