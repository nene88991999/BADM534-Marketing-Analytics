# Extended Reality in Business-to-Business Sales: Performance Implications, Adoption Drivers, and Contingency Effects

Janina Mettler; Aline Lanzrath; Christian Homburg. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429261443286).

Online publication: 2026-04-02. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Extended reality can improve B2B selling by supporting buyers' mental imagery, especially for complex and tactile products. Benefits coexist with possible salesperson stress. Adoption decisions should match the product and selling context and provide support for employees.

## Consumer Agency

Vivid simulation can create persuasive impressions beyond verified product performance. Distinguish demonstrations from validated capabilities.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: attention_salience, emotion_identity. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
