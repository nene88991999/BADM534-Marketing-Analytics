# Measuring Self-Preferencing on Digital Platforms

Lukas Jürgensmeier; Bernd Skiera. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251360772).

Online publication: 2025-07-11. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Platform self-preferencing requires a comparison with appropriately similar third-party offerings. Aggregate tests in the studied marketplaces find little evidence, while category and country results vary. Managers and regulators should examine sensitivity to comparison groups and visibility measures before drawing conclusions.

## Consumer Agency

The paper supplies a diagnostic for potentially biased visibility. Its reported aggregate result must not be described as evidence of pervasive self-preferencing.

Assessment: protective. Mechanism features: attention_salience, opacity_disclosure. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
