# EXPRESS: The Collecting Mindset: Viewing Purchasing as Collecting Enhances Acquisition Experience and Product Desirability

Elena Bocchi; Adam Eric Greenberg; Zachary Estes. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429261498190).

Online publication: 2026-10-09. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Positioning a purchase as part of a collection can improve the acquisition experience and product desirability. The effect depends on meaningful progress, active search, and identity fit. Managers should assess whether collecting improves the customer's experience within their intended budget.

## Consumer Agency

Collection completion and identity can encourage escalating acquisition. Preserve spending visibility and the freedom to leave a collection incomplete.
