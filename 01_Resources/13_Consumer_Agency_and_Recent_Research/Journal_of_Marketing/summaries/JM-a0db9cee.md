# Revenue Generation Through Influencer Marketing

Maximilian Beichert; Andreas Bayerl; Jacob Goldenberg; Andreas Lanz. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429231217471).

Online publication: 2024-02-09. Issue publication: 2024-07.

## Managerial Implications

In the studied direct-to-consumer campaigns, low-followership influencers deliver better endorsement returns than high-followership influencers after costs are considered. Managers should measure the full funnel through sales and endorsement spending, because follower reach alone can misstate economic performance.

## Consumer Agency

Commercial use of close follower relationships raises a risk when endorsements appear independent. Preserve sponsorship transparency and voluntary choice.
