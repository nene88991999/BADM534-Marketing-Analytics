# Putting Words in Their Mouths: Firm-Generated User Content and Consumer Sharing Behavior

Jonathan M. Beck; Clay M. Voorhees; Paul W. Fombelle; Katherine N. Lemon. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251331483).

Online publication: 2025-07-15. Issue publication: 2026-03.

## Managerial Implications

Providing ready-made social posts makes sharing easier but can discourage dissatisfied customers from sharing accounts that conflict with the supplied language. Managers should distinguish participation convenience from representative customer voice.

## Consumer Agency

Firm-written posts can appear spontaneous and suppress discordant experiences. Permit unrestricted editing and identify commercial assistance.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: defaults_order, opacity_disclosure, social_cues. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
