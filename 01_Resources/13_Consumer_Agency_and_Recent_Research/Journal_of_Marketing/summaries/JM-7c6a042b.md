# Consequences of Bottle Bills: How Bottle Deposit Return Schemes Affect Retail Prices and Lead Consumers to Larger Package Sizes

Kristopher O. Keller; Jonne Y. Guyt. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251347284).

Online publication: 2025-08-11. Issue publication: 2026-05.

## Managerial Implications

Bottle deposits can alter retail prices and package-size choices as well as recycling incentives. The New York policy analysis finds price and volume responses concentrated in covered products. Policy evaluation should include substitution across package sizes and operational costs.
