# Beyond Income: Dynamic Consumer Financial Vulnerability

Linda Court Salisbury; Gergana Y. Nenkov; Simon J. Blanchard; Ronald Paul Hill; Alexander L. Brown; Kelly D. Martin. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429221150910).

Online publication: 2023-04-18. Issue publication: 2023-09.

## Managerial Implications

Financial vulnerability varies over time and across income groups. Evaluate access to resources, volatility, and exposure to future harm across multiple periods. Income or general financial literacy alone may fail to identify customers who need support.

## Consumer Agency

A vulnerability framework can guide safeguards. Financial fragility must not become a targeting criterion for harmful commitments.

Assessment: protective. Mechanism features: financial_commitment. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
