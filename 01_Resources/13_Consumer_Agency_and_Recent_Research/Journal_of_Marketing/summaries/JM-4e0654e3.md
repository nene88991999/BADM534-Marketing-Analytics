# The Caring Machine: Feeling AI for Customer Care

Ming-Hui Huang; Roland T. Rust. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429231224748).

Online publication: 2024-03-22. Issue publication: 2024-09.

## Managerial Implications

AI customer care requires reliable emotion recognition, appropriate responses, and escalation. The conceptual framework links emotional support to both well-being and customer value. Managers should validate those outcomes separately and consult the related correction notice.

## Consumer Agency

Emotionally responsive agents can create dependence or deflect unresolved complaints. Disclose automation and retain independent escalation.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: emotion_identity, personalization, delegation_control. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
