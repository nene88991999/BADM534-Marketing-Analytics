# Collaborating to Innovate: Balancing Strategy Dividend and Transactional Efficiencies

Nehal Elhelaly; Sourav Ray. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429231222269).

Online publication: 2024-02-19. Issue publication: 2024-09.

## Managerial Implications

Supplier collaboration contracts should fit the firm's market positioning and capabilities. Misalignment can reduce innovation performance even when collaboration expands access to knowledge. Contract design therefore requires joint assessment of transaction hazards and the strategy the collaboration is intended to support.
