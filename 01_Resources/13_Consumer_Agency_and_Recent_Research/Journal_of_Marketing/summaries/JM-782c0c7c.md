# The Race for Data: Utilizing Informative or Persuasive Cues to Gain Opt-In?

Caterina D’Assergio; Puneet Manchanda; Elisa Montaguti; Sara Valentini. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429241288456).

Online publication: 2025-01-24. Issue publication: 2025-05.

## Managerial Implications

Data-consent messages combine information and persuasive cues in different ways, and those formats affect opt-in. Firms should evaluate understanding and freely expressed consent alongside participation. The observed prevalence of message formats concerns the studied email sample.

## Consumer Agency

Persuasive consent requests may increase agreement without better understanding. Make data uses, refusal, and withdrawal equally clear.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: privacy_control, opacity_disclosure, framing_reference. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
