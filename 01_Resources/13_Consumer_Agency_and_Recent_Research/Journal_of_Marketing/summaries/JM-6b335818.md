# All the News That's Fit to Capitalize: How Person Brands Shape Emergent Markets

Gillian Brooks; Ashlee Humphreys. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429261433005).

Online publication: 2026-03-01. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Prominent individuals can help emerging markets gain legitimacy through organizational identity, founding narratives, and early audiences. Managers should consider how these assets persist beyond the founding individual while checking the substance behind reputational claims.

## Consumer Agency

Personal authority and founding narratives may substitute for independent evidence. Preserve verification and the ability to question the spokesperson.
