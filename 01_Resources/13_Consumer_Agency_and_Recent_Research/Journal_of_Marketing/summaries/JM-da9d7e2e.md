# Before or After? The Effects of Payment Decision Timing in Pay-What-You-Want Contexts

Raghabendra P. KC; Vincent Mak; Elie Ofek. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429221142234).

Online publication: 2023-03-29. Issue publication: 2023-07.

## Managerial Implications

In pay-what-you-want settings, asking for payment after delivery can increase payment when experienced product value is high. The effect weakens or reverses at low value. Payment timing therefore requires attention to the experience delivered, including in charitable settings.

## Consumer Agency

Payment timing may increase felt reciprocity or obligation. Preserve a genuine ability to pay the amount the customer chooses.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: defaults_order, social_cues. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
