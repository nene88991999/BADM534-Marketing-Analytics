# Nudging App Adoption: Choice Architecture Facilitates Consumer Uptake of Mobile Apps

Crystal Reeck; Nathaniel A. Posner; Kellen Mrkva; Eric J. Johnson. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429221141066).

Online publication: 2023-03-13. Issue publication: 2023-07.

## Managerial Implications

App adoption responds to the sequence, grouping, color, and wording of choices. Bundling feature decisions can reduce their individual prominence, and wording can imply a default without formally preselecting an option. Evaluate understanding and feature-specific consent alongside onboarding completion.

## Consumer Agency

Increased adoption may reflect reduced attention to individual permissions. Preserve clear, separable choices and reversible settings.
