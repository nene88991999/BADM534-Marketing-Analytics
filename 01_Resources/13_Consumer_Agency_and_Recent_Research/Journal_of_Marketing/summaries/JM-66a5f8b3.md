# Sponsored Content as an Epistemic Market Object: How Platformization of Brand–Creator Partnerships Disrupts Valuation, Coproduction, and the Relationship Between Market Actors

Zeynep Arsel; Maria Carolina Zanette; Carolina da Rocha Melo. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429241296459).

Online publication: 2025-02-11. Issue publication: 2025-11.

## Managerial Implications

Brands and creators face uncertainty about sponsored content's value and production. Knowledge asymmetries can redistribute bargaining power and returns within these partnerships. Managers should make valuation criteria, responsibilities, and information access explicit.
