# Automated Versus Human Agents: A Meta-Analysis of Customer Responses to Robots, Chatbots, and Algorithms and Their Contingencies

Katja Gelbrich; Holger Roschk; Sandra Miederer; Alina Kerath. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251344139).

Online publication: 2025-08-26. Issue publication: 2026-03.

## Managerial Implications

Customers' responses to robots, chatbots, and algorithms depend on agent type and task conditions. Findings from one automated-agent category may fail to generalize to another. Deployment decisions should evaluate performance, customer response, and access to human service separately.

## Consumer Agency

Humanlike presentation may create misplaced trust or conceal automation. Identify the agent and retain meaningful human escalation.
