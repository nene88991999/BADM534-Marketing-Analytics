# Finding Goldilocks Influencers: How Follower Count Drives Social Media Engagement

Simone Wies; Alexander Bleier; Alexander Edeling. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429221125131).

Online publication: 2022-11-29. Issue publication: 2023-05.

## Managerial Implications

Influencer engagement has an inverted-U relationship with follower count in the studied campaigns. Reach gains can be offset by weaker perceived relationships. Content customization and brand familiarity change this trade-off, so follower count alone is an incomplete selection criterion.

## Consumer Agency

Perceived personal relationships can make paid recommendations seem independent. Clear sponsorship disclosure and substantive product information protect evaluation.
