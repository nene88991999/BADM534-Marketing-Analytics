# Beyond Visibility: The Disability Inclusion Effect in Advertising

Martina Cossu; Zachary Estes; Joachim Vosgerau. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429261447790).

Online publication: 2026-04-24. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Advertising that meaningfully includes people with disabilities can improve consumer evaluations across several settings. Tokenizing disability or emphasizing impairment weakens the effect. Representation should support agency and inclusion rather than use vulnerability as a promotional cue.

## Consumer Agency

The studies support representations that preserve agency and avoid tokenization.
