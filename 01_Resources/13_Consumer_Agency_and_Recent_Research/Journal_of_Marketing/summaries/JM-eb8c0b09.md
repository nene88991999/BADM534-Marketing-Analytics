# Customers’ Review Content and Their Referral and (Re)Purchase Behaviors

Ruhan Liu; Wanqing Zhang; Pradeep K. Chintagunta. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251352842).

Online publication: 2025-10-27. Issue publication: 2026-05.

## Managerial Implications

Writing a review can change the writer's later referral and purchase behavior. Affective and cognitive content produce different responses across the studied settings. Review requests should support honest reflection and measure subsequent behavior without substituting commercially convenient language for the customer's account.

## Consumer Agency

Shaping the sequence and content of reflection can change subsequent decisions. Allow customers to describe both feelings and reasons in their own words.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: emotion_identity, defaults_order. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
