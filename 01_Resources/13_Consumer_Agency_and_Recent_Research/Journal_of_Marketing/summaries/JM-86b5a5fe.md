# EXPRESS: Rethinking Imitation in Marketing Strategy

Suyun Mah; Neil A. Morgan. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429261469202).

Online publication: 2026-07-07. Issue publication: not recorded.

## Managerial Implications

Imitation can be a deliberate strategic option with different costs, benefits, and risks across market conditions. Managers should distinguish forms of imitation and compare them with innovation alternatives. The article develops a conceptual framework rather than estimating a universal performance advantage.
