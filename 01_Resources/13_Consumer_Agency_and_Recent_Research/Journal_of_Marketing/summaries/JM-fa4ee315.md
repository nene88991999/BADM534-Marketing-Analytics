# Social Profit Orientation: Lessons from Organizations Committed to Building a Better World

Leonard L. Berry; Tracey S. Danaher; Timothy Keiningham; Lerzan Aksoy; Tor W. Andreassen. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429241258495).

Online publication: 2024-07-26. Issue publication: 2025-03.

## Managerial Implications

Organizations pursuing social benefit need explicit objectives, resources, and implementation practices. Executive interviews identify conditions supporting this orientation across sectors. Managers should measure stakeholder outcomes directly rather than infer social benefit from conventional commercial performance.
