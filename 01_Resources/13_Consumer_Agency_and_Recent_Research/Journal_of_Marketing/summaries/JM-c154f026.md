# In-Store Advertising with Digital Signage

Dennis Herhausen; David de Jong; Dhruv Grewal. Journal of Marketing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/00222429251351578).

Online publication: 2025-11-07. Issue publication: 2026-05.

## Managerial Implications

Field experiments find that in-store digital signage can increase featured-product purchasing, with substantial context dependence and spillovers. Effects vary with product type, placement, and message. Evaluate incremental purchasing and customer outcomes alongside advertising revenue.

## Consumer Agency

Point-of-choice attention and emotional messaging may encourage purchases without improved product understanding. Preserve clear price and product information.

Assessment: potential dual use. Mechanism features: attention_salience, emotion_identity. A potential misuse is an analytical inference, not a claim about the authors' intentions or proof of harm.
