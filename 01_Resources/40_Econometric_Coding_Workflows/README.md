# Econometric Coding Workflows

A manager commissioning an analysis needs the intended calculation, an account of its uncertainty, and evidence that the program implements both. Galiani, López, and Sosa compare single-script chatbots, execution feedback with revision, and constrained agents on specified statistical tasks. Their November 2026 manuscript connects workflow design to success, error visibility, and computation cost.

- [Original course programs](../../03_Data_and_Code/Agent_Workflows/README.md) and [optional practice](../../07_Labs/Agent_Workflows/README.md).

The three small CSV files in `data/` are unchanged copies from the authors' v1.2 release. They contain published aggregates. The course programs recompute percentages and contrasts from their counts, preserve the supplied cluster-bootstrap intervals, and distinguish those results from constructed mHealth errors and hypothetical operating costs. The original 43,200 model runs have not been rerun for the course.
