# Earnings Calls and Pricing Communication

The [public teaching dataset and complete programs](../../../03_Data_and_Code/Earnings_Calls/README.md) contain numerical records, source URLs, dictionary patterns, and course-authored interpretation cases. They connect disclosure and speaking exposure to construct validity, event-window comparisons, and pricing communication.

The public export includes 2,326 call records and 971 match records. Its 123-firm roster is distinct from the number of firms with observed calls. The [data dictionary](../../../03_Data_and_Code/Earnings_Calls/DATA_DICTIONARY.md) defines the observation units and missingness; the [results](../../../03_Data_and_Code/Earnings_Calls/results/README.md) state the executed comparisons.

The supplied original collectors, excerpts, and figures remain local reference material. No open redistribution license was supplied for the excerpt collection. Students can reproduce every course analysis from the public export without the original bundle, scraping, or an account. The source history and transformation are recorded in the teaching dataset's manifest.
