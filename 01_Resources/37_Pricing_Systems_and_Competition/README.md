# Pricing Systems, Competition, and Customer Choice

A restaurant operator considering a pricing service must evaluate the information it supplies, the decisions it delegates, and the consequences for customers. Better demand forecasts can increase profit while reducing consumer surplus. Coordination among separate businesses raises an additional question about the independence of their pricing decisions.

## Wendy's Pricing Case

The November 23, 2026 complaint in *Garcia v. The Wendy's Company and NCR Voyix Corporation*, No. 8:26-cv-04886-LKG (D. Md.), alleges price fixing and unlawful information exchange through a shared transaction-data system. The [Reuters report](https://www.reuters.com/business/wendys-hit-with-us-class-action-over-menu-pricing-2026-11-24/) provides the news introduction. The [source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md) distinguishes allegations, company statements, economic research, and an agency's legal argument. The [case design](CASE_DESIGN.md) gives the discussion sequence and evidence questions.
