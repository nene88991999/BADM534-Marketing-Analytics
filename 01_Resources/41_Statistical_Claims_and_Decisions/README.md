# Statistical Claims and Marketing Decisions

A decision requires an effect, uncertainty, an economically meaningful comparison, and an account of affected people. These resources connect p-values, power, equivalence, and public claims to that decision.

## Sources and Course Uses

- [(Lakens, 2022, Chapter 1)](https://lakens.github.io/statistical_inferences/01-pvalue.html): definitions and interpretation of p-values. M3 §3.19 uses original store simulations; M6 §6.32 separates detection from campaign profit.
- [(Lakens, 2022, Chapter 9)](https://lakens.github.io/statistical_inferences/09-equivalencetest.html): interval hypotheses and equivalence. M9 §9.25 uses hypothetical cancellation-time summaries with full Python.
- [(Rana et al., 2026)](RanaKapsNetessine_WorkingPaper2026.pdf), *The Cost of Intelligence: Impacts of AI Data Centers on US Electricity Prices*: recurring case in M2 §2.28, M3 §3.19, M6 §6.33, M8 §8.31, and M9 §9.26. Read the [paper companion](PAPER_COMPANION.md) and [claim assessment](CLAIM_ASSESSMENT.md).
- [(Kost, 2026)](https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/blaming-data-centers-for-higher-electric-bills-its-not-that-simple): HBS public interpretation, used as an example of overstatement assessed against Table 4. The [communication guide](PUBLIC_COMMUNICATION_AND_PUBLICATION.md) provides two further readings and a premise-by-premise comparison.
- [(Thomasson, 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1093/9780197787830.003.0011) and [Norms and Necessity](https://academic.oup.com/book/33564): concepts, rules, and their functions. Contemporary applications distinguish measurements, household burdens, and authority.
- [(Kant, 1785)](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/5682/5682-h/5682-h.htm): universal law and humanity formulations; the course's public-reassurance application is an analytical argument.

The [programs](../../03_Data_and_Code/Statistical_Inference/README.md) reproduce the numerical examples used in Sessions 4, 9, and 14, and the [optional ungraded lab](../../07_Labs/Statistical_Inference/README.md) extends those comparisons. The [source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md) records each reading’s version, access, rights, and reading scope.

## Logical Arguments and Inverse Power

The [argument record](ARGUMENT_ASSESSMENT.md) connects the paper's claims to missing premises, counterexamples, and narrower supported conclusions. M9 §9.27 develops validity, soundness, quantifiers, extrapolation, and authority. M6 §6.34 implements [the inverse-power approach (Andrews, 1989)](https://doi.org/10.2307/1913623), with scalar thresholds and a synthetic joint-test example. Complete Python, aggregate outputs, and two editable TikZ figures accompany the notes.

## Institutional Exposure and Household Bills

The [institutional assessment](INSTITUTIONAL_ASSESSMENT.md) connects the project pipeline to treatment timing, donor eligibility, cost recovery, and the distinction between average prices and household expenditure. M6 §6.35 supplies complete synthetic Python examples and an editable diagram; M2 §2.28.1 and M9 §9.27.6 develop measurement and the missing-premise argument.

## Public Claims and Affected People

[Public Communication and Publication](PUBLIC_COMMUNICATION_AND_PUBLICATION.md) connects the HBS overstatement, statistical insignificance, pricing communication, and the decision to revise or publish a claim. M8 §8.31.1 and the optional lab distinguish nonrejection from a justified conclusion about harm. [Stakeholders and Reasons](../15_Marketing_Dialectics/07_Stakeholders_and_Reasons/README.md) supplies the philosophical reading guide.

## Measurement Validity and Ontological Scope

[M9 §9.42.4](../../06_Lecture_Notes/modules/M09_Marketing_Dialectics.md) maps the paper’s recorded objects to the broader claims that need validation. It separates realized revenue per MWh from bills and affordability, operating capacity from financial exposure, developer identity from business model, and an average from household incidence. The [philosophy and measurement guide](../15_Marketing_Dialectics/08_Logic_and_Analytical_Arguments/README.md) links the measurement-validity framework (Adcock and Collier, 2001) and the ontological spectrum. Complete bill calculations remain in M2 §2.28.1 and M6 §6.35.
