# Statistical Claims and Marketing Decisions

A decision requires an effect, uncertainty, an economically meaningful comparison, and an account of affected people. These resources connect p-values, power, equivalence, and public claims to that decision.

## Sources and Course Uses

- [Daniel Lakens, Chapter 1](https://lakens.github.io/statistical_inferences/01-pvalue.html): definitions and interpretation of p-values. M3 §3.19 uses original store simulations; M6 §6.32 separates detection from campaign profit.
- [Daniel Lakens, Chapter 9](https://lakens.github.io/statistical_inferences/09-equivalencetest.html): interval hypotheses and equivalence. M9 §9.25 uses hypothetical cancellation-time summaries with full Python.
- [Rana, Kaps, and Netessine (2026)](RanaKapsNetessine_WorkingPaper2026.pdf), *The Cost of Intelligence: Impacts of AI Data Centers on US Electricity Prices*: recurring case in M2 §2.28, M3 §3.19, M6 §6.33, M8 §8.31, and M9 §9.26. Read the [paper companion](PAPER_COMPANION.md) and [claim assessment](CLAIM_ASSESSMENT.md).
- [HBS Working Knowledge, November 12, 2026](https://www2.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/blaming-data-centers-for-higher-electric-bills-its-not-that-simple): verified public interpretation, distinguished from the paper's estimates and from unverified company dissemination.
- [Thomasson, Pragmatic Conceptual Engineering](https://doi.org/10.1093/9780197787830.003.0011) and [Norms and Necessity](https://academic.oup.com/book/33564): concepts, rules, and their functions. Contemporary applications distinguish measurements, household burdens, and authority.
- [Kant, Groundwork, second section](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/5682/5682-h/5682-h.htm): universal law and humanity formulations; the course's public-reassurance application is an analytical argument.

The [source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md) records rights, version, reading scope, and access. The [programs](../../03_Data_and_Code/Statistical_Inference/README.md) reproduce all numerical examples. The [lab](../../07_Labs/Statistical_Inference/README.md) is optional ungraded practice. Sessions 4, 9, and 14 use selected comparisons within existing meeting allocations.
