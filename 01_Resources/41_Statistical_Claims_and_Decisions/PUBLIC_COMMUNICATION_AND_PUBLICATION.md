# Public Communication and Publication

A research finding can influence policy through the public interpretation attached to it. The relevant decision is whether the evidence justifies publishing that interpretation and using it to allocate burdens or close a dispute. [M9 §§9.28–9.32](../../06_Lecture_Notes/modules/M09_Marketing_Dialectics.md) develops the philosophical argument; [M8 §8.31](../../06_Lecture_Notes/modules/M08_Current_Topics_and_Integrated_Decisions.md) examines research as public reassurance.

## HBS Coverage as an Example of Overstatement

The HBS Working Knowledge article gives an example of overstatement when it attributes no consumer electricity-cost increase to the four large developers [(Kost, 2026)](https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/blaming-data-centers-for-higher-electric-bills-its-not-that-simple). Its future-price qualification leaves the historical inference unresolved. The defect can be established by comparing that inference with the paper's own reported uncertainty.

Table 4's preferred residential specification reports a four-developer coefficient of 0.158 with clustered standard error 0.160. It uses 627 observations from 41 treated utilities. The coefficient measures dollars per MWh of average residential electricity price per percentage point of cumulative capacity relative to utility peak demand. A normal-approximation 95% interval is approximately [-0.156, 0.472] in those units. The direct developer-equality test has p=0.101 [(Rana et al., 2026, Table 4)](RanaKapsNetessine_WorkingPaper2026.pdf). The [complete calculation](../../03_Data_and_Code/Statistical_Inference/data_center_claims.py) and [M6 §6.33](../../06_Lecture_Notes/modules/M06_Causal_Inference_and_Experiments.md) preserve the estimand and units.

For the question of whether harm is absent or negligible, statistical insignificance alone has no determinate substantive interpretation. The same label can accompany very different intervals and economically consequential alternatives. A small-effect conclusion requires a justified margin and evidence excluding larger effects under an adequate design. Different subgroup significance levels do not establish a group difference. The evidence also leaves the opposite categorical claim, that these developers necessarily caused harm, unsupported.

## Compare Public Interpretations

TheEnergyMag explicitly distinguishes nonrejection from absence and treats the developer contrast as uncertain [(Zhao, 2026)](https://www.theenergymag.com/news/market-news/data-center-add-little-home-power-cost-study). This provides a useful comparison for evaluating whether a public interpretation preserves the inference available from the results. The [Washington Post coverage (Osaka, 2026)](https://www.washingtonpost.com/climate-environment/2026/11/06/data-centers-may-not-be-raising-americans-electricity-bills-that-much/) supplies a third reading for the same exercise.

For each article, record the speaker, date, exact claim, population, time horizon, and uncertainty. Distinguish the headline, the body, and any qualification. Reconstruct the premise needed to move from the reported test to the asserted conclusion. Retain qualifications beside the claims they constrain, then determine whether they address the same outcome and period. The [optional practice](../../07_Labs/Statistical_Inference/README.md#evaluate-public-reassurance-and-household-agency) connects that assessment to a household's reason for challenging public reassurance.

The three articles establish public communication about the study. Corporate dissemination, a coordinated campaign, and actual reliance in a policy decision require separate evidence. The hypothetical university-branded headline elsewhere in the philosophical discussion remains a constructed example.

## Whose Reasons Receive a Hearing?

Identify the actor, exact statement, audience, date, and supported scope. Trace whether a statement was used in a public decision before claiming that it displaced residents' objections. Distinguish nearby residents, utility customers, and people bearing a specified cost. Measure access to records, expert assistance, participation opportunities, and reasoned responses.

A resident's testimony can identify an outcome requiring investigation. A causal explanation needs an appropriate comparison. An average estimate can inform that comparison while leaving individual incidence or institutional authority unresolved. Reputation does not fill an evidentiary gap. A fair procedure ought to allow material objections to receive a substantive answer.

The [stakeholder guide](../15_Marketing_Dialectics/07_Stakeholders_and_Reasons/README.md) distinguishes Scanlonian reasonable rejection, moral standing, managerial salience, and epistemic justice. These accounts identify reasons to examine a process. Their application requires evidence about the particular people and institutions.

## Publish, Revise, Delay, or Decline?

Compare the contribution of the evidence with the claims proposed for release. A useful estimate may warrant publication with a narrower interpretation. A consequential unsupported claim may warrant correction or delay until its premises are examined. An already public error calls for a visible correction that preserves traceability. A paper's unfavorable implications for a company do not determine its quality, and neither do favorable implications.

[Mill's discussion of free inquiry in *On Liberty*, chapter II](https://dhspriory.org/kenny/PhilTexts/Mill/Liberty2.html) gives reasons to preserve criticism and contestable evidence. [Registered Reports](https://www.cos.io/initiatives/registered-reports) illustrate an institutional separation of design assessment from the direction of results. Neither requires publication of an unsupported interpretation. The practical question is which version, evidence, and review conditions justify release.

For the electricity case, students should state the defect precisely, demonstrate it using the paper's own numbers where possible, and explain why the proposed remedy addresses it. The argument evaluates evidence and public claims. It does not infer misconduct or intent from a methodological criticism.
