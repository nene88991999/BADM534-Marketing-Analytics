**Source:** PyMC Labs and contributors. [Open Resource](https://www.pymc-marketing.io/en/latest/notebooks/mmm/mmm_example.html). **Priority:** Optional. **Syllabus:** M8.

**Teaching Use:** Use a small example to examine how modeling assumptions affect spending recommendations.

**Conditions and Adaptation:** Full Bayesian estimation is unsuitable for the simplest browser-only lesson.
