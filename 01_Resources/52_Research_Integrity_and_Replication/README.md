# Research Integrity and Replication

An empirical recommendation deserves scrutiny of both its calculation and the records from which it is made. These cases connect data as an artifact to responsibility for collection, preservation, reporting, and correction. The [catalog](catalog.json) contains twelve primary-source records, with inspection scope and publication status checked November 27, 2026.

The 2024 author statement reports no evidence of falsification or knowing use of falsified data, alongside a conclusion that more preventive care was required. The full investigative report was not inspected. The 2026 journal retraction records agreement by both authors and lack of confidence in the underlying evidence. The course does not infer personal fabrication from coauthorship or publication status.

## Teaching Route

Begin with [M2 §2.35](../../06_Lecture_Notes/modules/M02_Customer_Data_and_Visualization.md) and the [complete code and licensed data](../../03_Data_and_Code/Research_Integrity/README.md). Define the unit, missingness, time intervals, and provenance before inspecting the results. [M9 §9.84](../../06_Lecture_Notes/modules/M09_Marketing_Dialectics.md) develops harms, correction, and limits on personal attribution. The [optional lab](../../07_Labs/Research_Integrity/README.md) asks students to defend an inference and assign responsibilities.

Data openness creates an opportunity for scrutiny; competent investigation and institutional action make it effective. Human protection can justify secure independent access. Provider-selected access and control of adverse publication raise different questions. The [corporate-standing readings](../45_Data_Truth_and_Norms/06_Corporate_Standing/README.md) and [Kilts policy case](../45_Data_Truth_and_Norms/06_Corporate_Standing/KILTS_CASE.md) develop that comparison without asserting a causal connection between those policies and the integrity scandals.

The distinction between computational reproducibility and new-data replication follows (National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, 2019), [chapter 3](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/25303/chapter/6). Responsibility and maxims are developed in M9 §§9.65.7–9.65.8 with (Nissenbaum, 1996), (Young, 2006), (Kant, 1785), and (Scanlon, 1998). A plausible account of downstream harm is not an estimated total loss.
