## R02: Personalized Pricing and Consumer Welfare

Jean-Pierre Dubé; Sanjog Misra. Downloaded NBER revision June 2021; published JPE 131(1), 2023; published tables cross-checked online.

**Reading:** Introduction; Sections 4–5; Table 7; published discussion and conclusion.
