## R01: Causal Alignment: Augmenting Language Models with a/B Tests

(Angelopoulos et al., 2026). Author PDF linked as August 2026 revision; author page lists acceptance at JMR.

**Reading:** Sections 1, 4–6; Table 2; Appendices A–B.

Within-experiment comparisons supervise language generation. Examine the chosen click outcome, retained human review, and factual-acceptability screening separately from customer benefit.

[Source](https://leekevin.io/caLM.pdf).

## R02: Personalized Pricing and Consumer Welfare

(Dubé and Misra, 2021, 2023). Downloaded NBER revision June 2021; published JPE 131(1), 2023; published tables cross-checked online.

**Reading:** Introduction; Sections 4–5; Table 7; published discussion and conclusion.

Distinguish the predicted optimal-price comparison from the later implemented field policies. Separate aggregate customer surplus, distributional incidence, and ethical interpretation.

[Source](https://www.nber.org/papers/w23775).

Local reference: `local_sources/Dube_Misra_WorkingPaper2021.pdf`. Original retained in Dropbox; public repository supplies attribution and original analysis.

## R04: Coarse Personalization

(Zhang and Misra, 2025). arXiv 2204.05793v4, June 30, 2025.

**Reading:** Introduction; segmentation formulation; application summary.

The size of a treatment menu and its commercial performance do not establish the ethical quality of the assignment rule. The authors discuss fairness and stability concerns.
