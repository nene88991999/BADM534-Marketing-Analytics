## P02: General Business Law §349-a: Pricing

New York State. Current text checked November 6, 2026.

**Reading:** Definitions, disclosure requirement, exceptions, enforcement.

Covers specified offers to consumers purchasing for personal, family, or household use. Keep this scope separate from business recruiting purchases.

[Source](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/349-A).
