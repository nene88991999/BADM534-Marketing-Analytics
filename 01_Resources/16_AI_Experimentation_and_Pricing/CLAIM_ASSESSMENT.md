# Claims, Evidence, and Ethical Inference

| Claim | Assessment and Supporting Scope |
| --- | --- |
| AI-trained messages increased the studied click outcome. | Supported by the field comparison in Causal Alignment. Preserve the campaign weighting, outcome, human-assisted workflow, and version. |
| The AI experiment demonstrated better customer welfare. | The reported click result supplies no corresponding customer-benefit estimand. Additional measurement and an ethical premise are required. |
| The messaging system omitted safeguards against misleading content. | Inconsistent with its retained human selection and Appendix B acceptability checks. Assess their coverage and effectiveness. |
| Personalized pricing can increase profit while lowering total customer surplus. | Supported in the pricing study's model-based comparison; the incidence and benchmark matter. |
| Every customer lost from personalization. | Inconsistent with the reported distributional results. The authors report benefits for a majority. |
| Lower aggregate surplus establishes a Kantian violation. | A further argument must identify the maxim, duty, and treatment of persons. A monetary aggregate is not Kantian autonomy. |
| A profit objective proves an author intends harm. | Unsupported inference about a person. Analyze the stated objective and particular recommendations. |
| The historical recruiting experiment violated current retail pricing rules. | Such a conclusion requires the relevant jurisdiction, date, transaction and statutory elements. Employer recruitment purchases differ from household-consumer transactions. |
| Deceptive urgency becomes permissible when a test shows increased sales. | A Kantian objection concerns the deceptive means. A performance estimate supplies no permission to disregard that duty. This is a hypothetical application. |

The phrase “marketing effectiveness at the cost of ethics” is a proposition to evaluate through a stated principle and evidence about conduct. The case retains findings that both support and constrain criticism. The commercial partnerships described by the papers motivate independent scrutiny, without establishing bias or personal misconduct.
