# Qualitative Evidence and Utility Cost Recovery

A household advocate needs to assess an assurance about electricity affordability. Utility disclosures supply evidence about planned recovery, timing, and claimed offsets. They also reflect an investor audience and the interests of the speaker. The [worked example](../../03_Data_and_Code/Utility_Cost_Recovery/README.md) connects three earnings calls to a filing announcement, an accounting disclosure, and a regulator's bill illustration. The [practice exercise](../../07_Labs/Qualitative_Evidence/README.md) asks students to revise an interpretation after examining adverse evidence.
